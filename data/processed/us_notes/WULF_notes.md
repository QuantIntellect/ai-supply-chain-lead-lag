# WULF — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only three calls (Q4-25 to Q2-26) are in the data.

- 2026-02-26 (Q4-25): $12.8bn of HPC leases signed in 2025, including a 450 MW lease with Fluidstack backed by Google's credit. Google's warrants will make it the largest shareholder. Acquired a 480 MW Kentucky campus. Contracting target raised to 250–500 MW a year through 2030. Data-center capex $8–10m/MW. Hyperscalers say power is the "binding constraint".
- 2026-05-08 (Q1-26; file dated 2026-06-01): HPC lease revenue $21m (+117% q/q). All Core42 capacity in service (CB-2), and CB-3 for Fluidstack/Google is on schedule. $3.1bn of cash after a $1.2bn equity raise. Customers want prompt power but will sign longer contracts.
- 2026-08-05 (Q2-26; file dated 2026-08-12): HPC lease revenue $31.9m (71% of revenue). CB-3 delivered, taking revenue-generating capacity to 102 MW and activating $600m of Google credit support. Signed a 401 MW lease with Anthropic in Kentucky (~$19bn over a 20-year term). Fluidstack leases amended for more than $500m of incremental revenue (CB-4/CB-5 raised to 168 MW each). Muskie campus could reach 2 GW and Chesapeake 1 GW; 2.9 GW pipeline in line of sight. Electrical labor is "highly constrained".

Current state (latest call): contracting is accelerating, with the Anthropic lease added to Fluidstack/Google and Core42, and the 250–500 MW/yr target reaffirmed. The binding constraints are electrical labor and power.
