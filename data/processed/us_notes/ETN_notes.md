# ETN — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: calls for Q4-21 to Q2-22 and Q3-25 to Q2-26 only; the Q3-2022 to Q2-2025 calls are missing from the data.

- 2022-02-04 (Q4-21): Electrical orders +21% rolling 12 months, Electrical backlog +56%. Data centers, distributed IT and industrial named as the strongest markets. 2022 organic guide 7–9%. Supply constraints limit service to "really strong" data-center and residential demand. Capex ~$650m (+$75m).
- 2022-05-03 (Q1-22): Electrical rolling-12-month orders +30% (vs +21%); another record backlog. Organic growth guide raised to 9–11% from 7–9%; supply chain still challenging.
- 2022-08-02 (Q2-22): segment orders +29% rolling 12 months and backlog +89% to a record. Data-center orders +25% rolling 12 months. Investing in "brick-and-mortar" capacity for the secular growth.
- 2025-11-04 (Q3-25; file dated 2025-11-05): data-center orders ~+70% y/y (Americas and Global), data-center sales +40%, data-center book-to-bill 1.7 (TTM). Electrical Americas backlog +20% to an all-time record. 2025 guide (8.5–9.5% organic) reaffirmed. Announced the Boyd Thermal liquid-cooling acquisition (~$1.7bn 2026 sales, more than 80% data center). Higher 2026 capex flagged.
- 2026-02-03 (Q4-25): Electrical Americas data-center orders ~+200%; Europe orders ~+80%. Electrical Americas backlog $13.2bn (+31%, record); combined book-to-bill above 1.2 for the quarter (1.1 TTM). 2026 organic guide 7–9%. Management noted hyperscalers "reconfirmed their CapEx plans for 2026"; 2025 data-center orders were about half cloud and half AI. Capacity-ramp costs are front-loaded.
- 2026-05-05 (Q1-26): data-center orders +240%. Electrical Americas rolling-12-month orders +42%, backlog +44%; book-to-bill 1.2 (record backlog). Organic guide raised 200bp to 9–11%, but the segment-margin guide was cut 50bp (ramp and input costs). US data-center backlog 228 GW (12 years at 2025 build rates). Capex above $1bn (record). Complete NVIDIA Vera Rubin solution; solid-state-transformer pilots with hyperscalers, with orders expected in H2 for late-2027 shipments.
- 2026-07-31 (Q2-26; file dated 2026-08-07): organic growth +18%, data-center sales ~+65% (eighth straight quarter above +35%). Electrical Americas record orders +41% rolling 12 months, book-to-bill 1.3; total company book-to-bill 1.2. Organic guide raised again to 11–13%; Boyd FY revenue raised to $1.8bn. US data-center backlog 307 GW (15 years). Price increase pulled forward to Q3. Electrical Americas backlog has grown by $5bn since the start of 2025 (+$700m q/q).

Current state (latest call): orders are still accelerating (Electrical Americas +41% LTM, record backlog, book-to-bill 1.3), and the 2026 organic guide has been raised twice to 11–13%. The constraint is factory ramp and margins, not demand.
