# CRM — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-03-01: FY23 revenue guide raised to $32.1bn at the high end (+21%), on "tremendous demand"; FY23 capex ~2% of revenue, "an all-time low".
- 2022-05-31: FY23 revenue guide cut $300m to $31.7–31.8bn on FX headwinds, while management still calls demand "very strong".
- 2022-08-24: Second cut, to $30.9–31.0bn (+17%), citing FX and "buyers being more measured" ("more deliberate customer buying behavior").
- 2022-11-30: Revenue guide held at $30.9–31.0bn. Operating-cash-flow growth fell to the low end (~16%) on lower billings, with pressure from tech-industry customers.
- 2023-03-01: FY24 revenue guide up to $34.7bn (~10% growth vs FY23 +18%); customer spending pressure in Commerce and Marketing; Einstein GPT announced.
- 2023-05-31: OpenAI named as a customer; $250m generative-AI venture fund launched; professional services see "less demand for multiyear transformations".
- 2023-08-30: FY24 revenue raised to $34.7–34.8bn (~11%). The AI fund, now $500m, backs Anthropic, Cohere and Hugging Face.
- 2024-02-28: FY25 revenue guide to $38bn at the high end (+9%) with no material gen-AI contribution assumed; 1,300 Einstein deals in FY24; capex slightly below 2% of revenue.
- 2024-05-29: FY25 subscription & support growth guide trimmed to ~10% cc (from slightly above 10%). Software bookings were hurt by go-to-market changes, and professional services were weak as customers delayed projects; cRPO $26.4bn (+10%).
- 2024-12-03: Agentforce (available Oct 24) already has 200+ deals, out of 2,000+ AI deals; $1m+ AI wins more than tripled y/y. FY25 guide low end raised to $37.8bn ($37.8–38.0bn); no material Agentforce contribution to cRPO expected in Q4.
- 2025-02-26: FY26 revenue guide $40.5–40.9bn (+7–8%). Expanded Google partnership (Agentforce with Gemini, Salesforce on Google Cloud); "AWS is a huge growth engine"; infrastructure "substrates" are live on Amazon and Alibaba. (The four FY26 calls, May 2025 to Feb 2026, are not in the data.)
- 2026-05-27: Agentforce ARR >$1bn; AI + Data ARR $3.4bn; 28.6tn tokens processed (+152% q/q). FY27 revenue guide raised to $45.9–46.2bn; cRPO $33.6bn (+14%). OpenAI, Anthropic and Google are described as Salesforce/Slack customers; McAfee is replacing ServiceNow with Agentforce IT service.
- 2026-08-26: Agentforce ARR >$1.5bn (+240% y/y) and AI + Data ARR ~$3.9bn (+210%). AI bookings "grew triple digit" (doubled y/y), with half from customers "refilling the tank" on flex credits. FY27 guide raised $300m to $46.1–46.4bn ($200m of it from the Contentful and Fin acquisitions). Claudeforce launched with Anthropic; multi-billion-dollar US Army expansion.

Current state (latest call): Core growth is ~11–12% (FY27 guide $46.1–46.4bn, cRPO +14%), but AI monetization is inflecting: Agentforce ARR >$1.5bn, AI + Data ARR ~$3.9bn (+210%) and token volume compounding quickly. CRM is now a sizable buyer of frontier-model inference (Anthropic and OpenAI partnerships).
