# WDC - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-01-27 to 2026-04-30
(16 calls in sample; HDD and Flash combined until the SanDisk separation in early 2025 (see SNDK notes); no calls between 2025-01-29 and 2025-10-30; the FQ4 FY26 call (2026-07/08) is not in the sample)

- 2022-01-27: Cloud revenue up 89% YoY and customers signal strong demand through CY22, but component shortages leave "a significant amount of unmet demand". FQ3 guide $4.45-4.65bn. FY22 gross capex ~$3bn, with cash capex trimmed to ~$1.5bn.
- 2022-04-28: Nearline revenue up nearly 40% YoY on 18/20TB cloud drives, and big cloud customers signal a strong 2H CY22. Long-term agreements (LTAs) are used to get visibility two to three quarters out. FQ4 guide $4.5-4.7bn. FY22 capex trimmed again (gross ~$2.9bn, cash ~$1.3bn).
- 2022-08-05: Peak and turn. Near-record nearline shipments of 111 EB and FY22 cloud revenue +40% (nearline +38%). At the same time an "aggressive inventory adjustment" hits PC OEMs (Flash leads the decline) and China cloud starts digesting while US hyperscalers hold. FQ1 FY23 guide $3.6-3.8bn vs FQ4's $4.5bn.
- 2022-10-27: Digestion reaches the hyperscalers: HDD ASPs fall as "hyperscale guys go through the digestion phase". NAND pricing -17% like-for-like (-22% blended). Client HDD capacity cut ~40%; FY23 gross capex cut to $2.7bn and cash capex -20%. FQ2 guide $2.9-3.1bn (FQ1 $3.7bn).
- 2023-01-31: Cloud inventory digestion "intensified" and enterprise SSD demand is "sharply reduced" as large cloud customers enter digestion. Flash wafer starts cut 30%. FY23 gross capex ~$2.3bn vs a $3.2bn plan, cash capex ~$900M. FQ3 guide $2.6-2.8bn. Apollo and Elliott invest $900M of convertible preferred.
- 2023-07-31: Trough. Cloud revenue $1 billion (37% of total, -53% YoY) and nearline shipments 59 EB (-26% QoQ). FY24 capex to "decline significantly". FQ1 FY24 guide $2.55-2.75bn. Management expects cloud customers back toward the end of FY24.
- 2024-01-25: Inflection: "multi-quarter nearline demand headwinds have subsided" as major cloud customers re-engage. Cloud revenue $1.1bn (+23% QoQ), nearline 67 EB, China hyperscalers returning, and price increases expected. FQ3 guide $3.2-3.4bn.
- 2024-04-25: Now "a supply constrained environment". Cloud revenue $1.6bn (+45% QoQ), nearline 108 EB. Customers give more ordering visibility, and AI is cited as a new storage growth engine for HDD and Flash. FQ4 guide $3.6-3.8bn.
- 2024-07-31: WDC now asks customers for 52 weeks of visibility (a 52-week HDD lead time). Cloud is 50% of revenue at $1.9bn (+21% QoQ, on volume and price). PCIe Gen5 and 64TB SSDs launched for AI. FQ1 FY25 guide $4-4.2bn.
- 2024-10-24: Cloud is 54% of revenue ($2.2bn, more than double YoY); enterprise SSD revenue +76% QoQ; record nearline shipments of 141 EB. NVIDIA qualified WDC's PCIe Gen5 SSD in its GB200 reference architecture, and the FY enterprise SSD bit mix is now seen at 15-20% vs ~10% expected. Management sees no excess customer inventory or double ordering. FQ2 guide $4.2-4.4bn.
- 2025-01-29: A split market before the SanDisk separation. HDD demand exceeds supply (record 154 EB nearline; cloud 55% of revenue at $2.3bn), while Flash faces short-term oversupply and client inventory digestion into 1H CY25. Combined FQ3 guide $3.75-3.95bn, with Flash revenue down mid-teens %.
- 2025-10-30: First HDD-only call in the sample: revenue $2.8bn (+27% YoY), 204 EB (+23%), cloud 89% of revenue. The top 7 customers have POs through 1H CY26 (5 of them through all of CY26), and one hyperscaler has an agreement covering all of CY27. CY26 will be "very supply constrained", with new exabytes mostly arriving in 2H CY27. FQ2 guide $2.9bn +/-$100M (~+20% YoY).
- 2026-01-29: "Pretty much sold out for calendar '26." Commercial agreements with 3 of the top 5 customers (2 through CY27, 1 through CY28). Revenue $3bn (+25%), 215 EB (+22%). FQ3 guide $3.2bn (~+40% YoY). HAMR and next-gen ePMR qualifications have started with hyperscalers.
- 2026-04-30: Revenue $3.3bn (+45% YoY); cloud $3.0bn (89% of revenue, +48%); 222 EB (+34%); pricing +9% YoY; gross margin >50%. Agentic AI is expected to drive a step-function in capacity storage demand, with long-term growth >25% CAGR. FQ4 guide $3.65bn (+40% YoY). HAMR is in qualification with 4 customers. Capacity is being added through heads and media, "not unit capacity". The SanDisk stake was monetized (5.8M shares, $3.1bn of debt reduction).
Current state (latest call): 2026-04-30. Nearline HDD is sold out for CY26, with POs and LTAs reaching 2027-28 and pricing up 9% YoY. Revenue is guided to $3.65bn (+40% YoY) at gross margins above 50%. Supply grows only through areal density (ePMR now, HAMR ramping in 2027), not new unit capacity.
