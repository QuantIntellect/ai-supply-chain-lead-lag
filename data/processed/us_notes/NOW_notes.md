# NOW — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-07-27: FY22 subscription-revenue guide trimmed to $6.915–6.925bn, mainly on an incremental $87m FX headwind. In the last weeks of June, deals elongated because they needed "higher levels of authority" to approve, more so in Europe. Co-sell arrangement with Microsoft Azure.
- 2023-01-25: FY23 subscription-revenue guide $8.44–8.5bn (+22.5–23.5%), having "prudently factored in the evolving macro crosswinds".
- 2023-07-26: Gen-AI push begins: AI Lighthouse customer program with NVIDIA and Accenture. FY23 subscription guide raised $95m (midpoint) to $8.58–8.6bn; cRPO +24% cc.
- 2023-10-25: FY23 guide raised again, by $48m to $8.635–8.640bn. NVIDIA is "accelerating its own ServiceNow journey with generative AI"; 1,789 customers above $1m ACV.
- 2024-01-24: RPO ~$18bn (+27.5% cc), cRPO $8.6bn (+23% cc). Record 10 new customers above $1m NNACV, including a $10m deal that is the largest new logo ever. FY24 subscription outlook $10.555–10.575bn (+$165m vs the prior outlook). Listed on AWS Marketplace.
- 2024-07-24: FY24 guide raised a second time (+$33m, beyond FX); cRPO $8.78bn (+22.5% cc, 150bp acceleration vs Q1); plans for a UAE cloud hosted on Microsoft Azure.
- 2024-10-23: FY24 subscription guide raised $78m to $10.655–10.66bn (+23%); cRPO $9.36bn (+23.5% cc); Now Assist sees "robust demand".
- 2025-01-29: FY25 subscription guide $12.635–12.675bn (+20% cc, down from ~23% in FY24). Gen-AI NNACV "stepped up meaningfully" in Q4, and customers buying two or more gen-AI products doubled q/q. The gross-margin guide of 83.5% reflects data-center investment for "public cloud partners, geo expansion, and AI". (The Q1-2025 call, April 2025, is not in the data.)
- 2025-07-23: FY25 subscription guide raised $125m to $12.775–12.795bn; 89 deals above $1m NNACV (11 above $5m); Now Assist NNACV beat expectations.
- 2025-10-29: FY25 guide raised $55m to a midpoint of $12.840bn (+20.5%). Now Assist ACV on pace for >$500m by year-end 2025, with consumption up 55x since May; 103 deals above $1m. Capex is lower "as some of our workloads move to hyperscalers"; the platform is built on NVIDIA Nemotron.
- 2026-01-28: FY26 subscription guide $15.53–15.57bn (+19.5–20% cc). Now Assist ACV >$600m (more than doubled y/y); cRPO +21% cc. The gross-margin headwind comes mostly from "moving more towards hyperscalers". Expanded partnerships with Microsoft, Anthropic and OpenAI (OpenAI models are "preferred" for several agentic use cases).
- 2026-04-22: FY26 subscription guide raised $205m to $15.735–15.775bn (includes 125bp from Armis). 2026 AI commitments now expected at $1.5bn, up from $1bn. RPO $27.7bn (+23.5% cc), cRPO $12.64bn (+21% cc); new-logo ACV growth >50%.

Current state (latest call): Steady ~20–21% cc subscription growth with guidance raised. AI monetization is scaling (Now Assist ACV >$600m at Q4-25; 2026 AI commitments target $1.5bn), and NOW is deliberately shifting hosting from its own data centers to hyperscalers (lower capex, gross-margin headwind), which adds demand for hyperscaler capacity.
