# FN - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2025-04-30 to 2026-08-17
(5 calls in sample; no calls before 2025-04 and none for 2025-08 (FQ4 FY25). Fiscal year ends June.)

- 2025-04-30: Datacom air pocket at the largest customer. Datacom revenue fell to $251M (-18% YoY, -16% QoQ) on "product transitions at a large customer", and 800G-and-faster revenue fell 8% as capacity was converted to 1.6T, which is destined for the customer's Blackwell Ultra. Telecom grew +42% YoY on 400ZR DCI. FQ4 guide $860-900M (~18% FY25 growth).
- 2025-04-30: First direct hyperscaler deal: a multi-year manufacturing agreement with Amazon Web Services that includes a warrant for up to 1% of FN shares, with revenue ramping in FY26.
- 2025-11-03: Record revenue of $978M (+22% YoY). DCI revenue nearly doubled to 14% of revenue, and datacom declined less than feared. First revenue ($15M) from a new HPC program. Capex ran above maintenance level for Building 10. FQ2 guide $1.05-1.1bn (+29% YoY at midpoint); "the demand is so strong".
- 2026-02-02: Record revenue of $1.13bn (+36% YoY, +16% QoQ). HPC revenue was $86M (the program is seen above $150M when fully ramped; FN is second source), and DCI was $142M. Datacom grew 2% QoQ, with more sequential growth expected. FQ3 guide $1.15-1.2bn (~35% YoY). Capex $52M for Building 10 and Pinehurst; demand is "strengthening".
- 2026-05-04: Record revenue of $1.214bn (+39% YoY), but datacom fell 6% QoQ to $260M on laser/memory/ASIC shortages: "not demand risk; it is supply constraints". Without them, datacom would have set a record "by a wide margin". DCI $197M (+90% YoY). Capacity is ~$4.8bn of revenue; Building 10 adds ~$3.0bn, and full build-out gives ~$11.5bn. FQ4 guide $1.25-1.29bn (~40%). FN paid $32M for ~14% of Raytec (CPO packaging).
- 2026-08-17: Record FQ4 revenue of $1.316bn (+45% YoY). Data-center revenue was $669M (51% of revenue, +68% YoY), with DCI at a $1bn run rate. FY26 revenue $4.64bn (+36%). FQ1 FY27 guide $1.375-1.425bn (+43% YoY).
- 2026-08-17: 10% customers in FY26 were Cisco 20%, NVIDIA 16%, Nokia 11% and Amazon 11%. Customers give visibility to the end of 2027 and beyond ("a staggering demand picture"). Capacity is a $5.3bn run rate, with land and plans for $12.5-14bn. Inventory rose to $1.02bn ahead of ramps.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-17 - record FQ4 revenue of $1.316bn and an FQ1 FY27 guide of $1.375-1.425bn (+43% YoY). Data center is now 51% of revenue, customer visibility runs past end-2027, and capacity is being expanded toward $12.5-14bn. Component supply (lasers, memory, ASICs), not demand, has been the gating factor for datacom.
