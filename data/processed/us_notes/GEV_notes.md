# GEV — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: only four calls are in the corpus (Q2-2025 to Q1-2026); three are filed under 2026-04-22, and the actual call dates are used below. Earlier GE Vernova calls are missing.

- 2025-07-23: Q2-25. Gas Power contracted ~9 GW in the quarter (2 GW into backlog, 7 GW into slot-reservation agreements). Backlog was flat at 29 GW, SRAs rose 21→25 GW, and backlog+SRAs reached 55 GW (50 GW in April). Management still expects ≥60 GW by YE25, with "higher turbine prices and strong demand". Power orders +44% (gas equipment nearly 3x y/y); total backlog $129bn.
- 2025-07-23: Raised FY25 FCF guidance by ~$1bn to $3.0–3.5bn "due to higher down payments from increased orders" and the Power EBITDA-margin guide to 14–15% (from 13–14%). Aeroderivative demand is rising "particularly to support data centers"; "very productive conversations with the hyperscalers".
- 2025-10-22: Q3-25. 12 GW of new gas contracts (after 9 GW in Q2): backlog 29→33 GW, SRAs 25→29 GW, total 62 GW. The YE25 target moved up to ~70 GW. Orders $14.6bn (+55%, book-to-bill ~1.5x); equipment backlog $54bn (+$11bn YTD). SRAs carry higher price and margin than the order book.
- 2025-10-22: Electrification booked $400m of hyperscaler orders in Q3, ~$900m YTD vs $600m in all of 2024. Signed a first technology-collaboration funding agreement with a hyperscaler for scope inside the data center. Organic growth guide raised to ~25% (from ~20%). Agreed to buy the remaining 50% of Prolec GE (transformers) from Xignux.
- 2026-01-28: Q4-25. 24 GW of new gas contracts in Q4 (6 GW in the last 3 weeks of December). Backlog+SRAs rose 62→83 GW (backlog 33→40, SRAs 29→43) vs ~60 GW expected mid-year. Target ~100 GW under contract in 2026, at which point 2029–30 would be "largely sold out". SRAs are priced 10–20 points above the existing backlog. FY25 orders $59bn (+34%); total backlog $150bn (+$31bn); equipment backlog $64bn.
- 2026-01-28: Electrification orders signed directly for data centers exceeded $2bn in 2025, more than triple 2024. Q4 Electrification orders ~$7.4bn (+50%, ~2.5x revenue). The first inside-the-data-center product goes to a hyperscaler in autumn 2026, with a possible order in 2027. Raised FY26 revenue guidance to $44–45bn (from $41–42bn, incl. Prolec).
- 2026-04-22: Q1-26. Signed 21 GW (US, Vietnam, Mexico, Brazil, Canada), taking gas under contract 83→100 GW (backlog 40→44, SRAs 43→56) across ~90 customers. About 20% of the 100 GW is direct to data centers. Expects 10–15 GW more in Q2 and ≥110 GW by YE26. Only ~10 GW is left across 2029 and 2030 combined; 2030 slots were signed, including with hyperscalers. 2026 orders are priced 10–20 points above Q4-25 on $/kW. Factory output ~20 GW/yr from mid-2026, 24 GW in 2028.
- 2026-04-22: Orders $18.3bn (+71%, book-to-bill ~2); backlog $163bn. Electrification orders $7.1bn (+86%), including ~$2.4bn from data centers, more than all of 2025. Raised FY26 revenue to $44.5–45.5bn (+$0.5bn), adjusted EBITDA margin to 12–14%, and Electrification revenue to $14.0–14.5bn.

Current state (latest call, 2026-04-22): Gas-turbine demand is still accelerating. 100 GW is under contract, heading to ≥110 GW, with 2029–30 nearly sold out and pricing up 10–20 pts. Data centers take ~20% of gas GW directly and $2.4bn/quarter of electrification orders. Guidance has been raised at each of the four calls in the corpus.
