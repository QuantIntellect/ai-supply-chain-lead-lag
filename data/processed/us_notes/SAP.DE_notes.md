# SAP.DE — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: one call in the corpus (Q3-2026).

- 2026-10-21: Q3-26. Current cloud backlog €24.1bn (+26% cc, stable vs Q2 and slightly accelerating organically). Cloud revenue €6.7bn (+25% cc, ~1 pt from one-offs). AI is "the main driver" of customers' moves to the cloud: 9 out of 10 of the 50 largest deals included AI and Business Data Cloud. Total revenue €10.3bn (+12%), with services −5%.
- 2026-10-21: Outlook. Reaffirmed current-cloud-backlog, operating-profit and FCF guidance, and is trending to the upper end of the cloud and software revenue outlook. Still expects a "slight deceleration" in cloud-backlog growth in 2026. AI-driven adoption support will keep pressuring billable services hours into next year.

Current state (latest call, 2026-10-21): AI-led cloud demand is steady (backlog +26%) with guidance at the upper end, a slight backlog-growth deceleration still expected for 2026, and AI cannibalizing services revenue.
