# EQIX — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-02-16: Record Americas bookings and record enterprise-vertical bookings. Initial 2022 guide: revenue +9–10% (above the long-term range) and capex ~$2.3–2.6bn. xScale hyperscale JVs were sized at >$8bn, 36 facilities and >720MW when built. Singapore was still capacity-constrained, though the government had lifted its build moratorium.
- 2022-04-27 to 2022-11-02: Three straight record-bookings quarters: best net bookings in company history in Q1-22, then record gross and net bookings in Q2-22 and Q3-22. Management saw "no waning of demand" despite the macro backdrop and raised list prices on space, power and interconnection. 2022 capex went $2.3–2.6bn → $2.3–2.5bn (Apr) → $2.3–2.6bn (Jul) → $2.1–2.3bn (Nov), the last cut attributed to "timing of expansion spend".
- 2023-02-15: Passed the full power-cost increase to customers from Jan 1, worth ~$350m of 2023 revenue (~500bp of reported growth). Guided 2023 revenue +9–10% excluding the pass-through, and 2023 capex $2.7–2.9bn. xScale leased ~8MW (Tokyo/Osaka).
- 2023-08-02: AI enters the demand narrative: an "accelerating appetite for companies to rapidly integrate AI" is driving demand for data-center capacity. 53 projects under way, including 11 xScale builds (~90MW). Power availability could constrain supply "market by market" but not EQIX's retail build plan.
- 2023-10-25: Management cited a "meaningful acceleration in demand for training" reshaping US supply and demand. Tight supply across many metros gave pricing power. Called CoreWeave and Lambda an "emergent set of service providers" that are sources of incremental demand.
- 2024-02-14: Record xScale leasing quarter driven by AI (90MW since the prior call; 300MW cumulative). Retail showed cross-currents: "more deliberate buying decisions and slightly higher levels of churn". The 2024 guide slowed to 7–8% normalized revenue growth (vs ~9% ex-power in 2023); 2024 capex $2.8–3.0bn.
- 2024-05-09: Highest Q1 bookings on record. Hyperscalers generated >$1.3bn of annualized retail revenue. xScale had ~350MW leased (~90% of operational plus under-construction capacity). First US xScale JV, with PGIM, for SV12x; the xScale portfolio reached >$8bn and >725MW.
- 2024-08-07 to 2024-10-30: Record gross bookings in Q2 and Q3-24. 2024 capex raised to $2.8–3.1bn and FY24 revenue guide raised $36m. Agreed a >$15bn xScale JV with CPP Investments and GIC, nearly tripling xScale capital; 385MW leased. Because of supply constraints, EQIX is now "preselling into future development sites".
- 2025-02-12: Best gross bookings in the company's 26-year history (both Q4 and FY24), and bookings "could have" been higher with Tier-1 capacity. Largest backlog in three years. xScale leased ~150MW in 2024 (>400MW cumulative). Under "Build Bolder", NY3, DC16 and LD4 were pulled forward by at least a year. 2025 capex $3.2–3.5bn. The data has no Q1-25 or Q2-25 call.
- 2025-10-29: Record annualized gross bookings of $394m (+25% y/y), plus $185m pre-sold, with "demand continuing to outpace supply". Developable capacity rose to ~3GW (+~50% q/q, including >900MW of new land). Power is not a constraint for the 12 xScale projects. 2025 capex raised to $3.8–4.3bn.
- 2026-02-11: Q4-25 gross bookings $474m (+42% y/y); FY25 $1.6bn (+27%). ~60% of the largest deals were AI-driven, up from ~50% earlier in 2025, and the cabinet backlog is at a record. The Hampton xScale campus (~240MW; half to be leased to a hyperscaler) slipped to Q1. 2026 capex guided $3.7–4.2bn, and ~1GW of the 3GW land bank is earmarked for xScale.
- 2026-04-29: Q1 gross bookings $378m (+9%) plus $140m of pre-sales: total sales activity +35% and a record backlog. 8 of the top-10 AI model providers and 4 of the top-5 neoclouds are expanding with EQIX (110+ network nodes). Revenue, EBITDA and AFFO guides raised; capex now at the top of the range (~$4.1bn). "The availability of power would be the largest constraint".
- 2026-07-29: Gross bookings $424m (+23%) plus ~$110m of pre-sales; record backlog. Closed 134MW of xScale leases including Hampton (~$120m of fees). 2026 revenue guide raised $100m (to 11–12% growth). 2026 capex raised to $5–6bn (ex land and xScale), with $5–7bn a year planned for 2027–29 and revenue growth of 10–13% a year. "Demand is clearly exceeding the assumptions in our prior long term outlook."

Current state (latest call 2026-07-29): accelerating. Bookings, pre-sales and backlog are at or near records, and most large deals are AI workloads. Capex is being roughly doubled versus 2024 ($5–6bn in 2026; $5–7bn a year through 2029), and power availability in the top metros is the binding constraint.
