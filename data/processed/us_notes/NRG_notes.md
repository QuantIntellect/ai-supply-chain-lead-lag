# NRG — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: four calls (Q3-2025 to Q2-2026). The Q4-25 call (2026-02-24) is filed under 2026-05-06.

- 2025-11-06: Q3-25. Added 150 MW of data-center PPAs, taking contracted capacity to 445 MW across ERCOT and PJM; the latest deal signed above the midpoint of the $70–90/MWh target. Raised the target for new data-center agreements to >$80/MWh. LOI/joint-development pipeline 5.4 GW; up to 5.4 GW of new capacity through 2032 via GE Vernova/Kiewit. The LS Power deal is pending (with Rockland, TEF projects and VPP: +15 GW gas and 7 GW VPP). Core 14% EPS CAGR even with no data centers.
- 2026-02-24: Q4-25. LS Power closed (January 30). Targets ≥1 GW of signed long-term data-center contracts in 2026 under "Bring Your Own Power". More than 6 GW of gas capacity is reserved for customer-backed large-load projects (5.4 GW GEV/Kiewit plus ~1 GW of uprates in the LS Power fleet).
- 2026-05-06: Q1-26. Reaffirmed 2026 guidance, which needs no large-load contribution. The preliminary long-term load forecast shows >36 GW of large-load requests by 2033. Large-load talks are "active and progressing", focused on front-of-the-meter.
- 2026-08-04: Q2-26. Aligned on principal terms with "the leading global cloud and AI hyperscaler" for a 1.2 GW Texas new-build (1 GW data-center load; could expand to 2.4 GW). 95% of project FCF comes from capacity payments regardless of data-center utilization. Capex $3.2bn ($2,700/kW; $721m in 2026); ≥$500m EBITDA and ~$375m FCF at full operation; COD late 2029.
- 2026-08-04: The development pipeline is more than twice the 5.4 GW of secured turbine and EPC slots, "every turbine slot tied to an active customer discussion". Softer Texas load and prices plus Winter Storm Fern costs have the year tracking below the guidance midpoint.

Current state (latest call, 2026-08-04): First hyperscaler new-build deal (1.2 GW, capacity-payment structure) is agreed in principle, the pipeline exceeds 10 GW against 5.4 GW of secured turbines, and the near-term Texas power market is soft.
