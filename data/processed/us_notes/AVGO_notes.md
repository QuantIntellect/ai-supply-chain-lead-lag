# AVGO - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-03-03 to 2026-09-02
(18 calls in sample; no call between 2025-06-05 and 2025-12-11, i.e. the Sep-2025 Q3 FY25 call is missing)

- 2022-03-03: Pre-AI supply-tight cycle: hardware backlog >$25bn (vs $22bn prior qtr), lead times steady at 50 weeks and already "booking ... in '23"; channel inventory ~one month; Q2 semis guided +25% YoY; wafer/substrate/assembly cost increases passed to customers. Hyperscale custom compute-offload chips and Jericho in the Arista 7800 AI platform already cited.
- 2022-12-08: First hard AI number: custom compute-offload accelerators "surpassed the $2 billion amount in revenues" in FY22; networking $2.5bn (+30% YoY) on Tomahawk 4 at hyperscalers. Q1 guide "pretty flat", backlog/lead-time figures no longer given; enterprise IT budgets not seen dropping below 2022.
- 2023-03-02: Gen-AI inflection call: AI Ethernet switch shipments >$200M in 2022, forecast "well over $800 million" in 2023 on "expected exponential demand" from hyperscalers, who show a "sense of urgency ... to not miss out". Still booked for FY23 with ~50-week lead times; gen AI also driving the compute-offload (custom accelerator) business.
- 2023-06-01: Gen-AI revenue ~15% of semis (10% in FY22) and "could be over 25%" in FY24. Demand "unabated", but ramp "more measured" because leading-edge wafers, substrates, packaging and memory stacking are tight. Non-AI enterprise moderating (Q3 server-storage connectivity only up low-single digits).
- 2023-08-31: "Substantial orders" for Tomahawk 5 / Jericho3-AI; Q4 gen-AI networking guided ~+50% QoQ and ~2x YoY (networking >20% YoY). "A lot of constraints" across chips, packaging and HBM; non-AI semis stable at ~$6bn/qtr ("soft landing").
- 2023-12-07: Q4 FY23 gen-AI revenue close to $1.5bn (20% of semis; FY23 15%), driven by custom AI accelerators and Ethernet at hyperscalers. FY24 guide: gen AI >25% of semis, networking +30%; non-AI flat at ~$6bn.
- 2024-03-07: FY24 AI outlook raised to >$10bn (~35% of semis, from >25%); Q1 AI $2.3bn (4x YoY); networking guide raised to >35% from 30%; custom accelerators at "our two hyperscale customers". Non-AI guides cut (server storage down mid-20s% vs high-teens; broadband -30% vs mid-teens). "We do not have a supply chain constraint" thanks to hyperscaler lead time.
- 2024-06-12: Q2 AI $3.1bn (+280% YoY); FY24 AI raised to >$11bn, networking to +40%; "awarded the next-generation custom AI accelerators" at its hyperscale customers; ~25% of GPU-cluster value goes to networking. Broadband cut again (down high-30s); semis GM -370bp on custom-accelerator mix.
- 2024-09-05: FY24 AI raised to $12bn (Q4 AI >$3.5bn, +10% QoQ) and FY24 revenue to $51.5bn. Non-AI "reached bottom" with non-AI bookings +20% YoY; "getting pull-ins ... upsides", expected "a lot more in 2025". XPUs in production at three customers.
- 2024-12-12: FY24 AI $12.2bn (+220%, 41% of semis); Q4 AI $3.7bn (+150%) on 2x XPU shipments and 4x AI connectivity. New framing: three hyperscale XPU customers make a $60-90bn AI SAM in FY27 (1M-XPU clusters); two more hyperscalers selected for next-gen XPUs. Q1 FY25 AI guided $3.8bn (+65%).
- 2025-06-05: Q2 AI >$4.4bn (+46%), with AI networking at 40% of AI revenue (+70% YoY); Q3 AI guided $5.1bn (+60%). Visibility into FY26 XPU deployments "much more than we originally thought"; "tremendous demand" for the 102T Tomahawk 6.
- 2025-12-11: Order-book step change: >$73bn AI backlog to ship over 18 months (almost half of the $162bn total backlog); AI-switch backlog >$10bn. Anthropic ordered $10bn of TPU Ironwood racks (Q3 FY25) plus $11bn more for late-2026 delivery; fifth XPU customer ($1bn order); OpenAI 10GW agreement for 2027-29. FY25 AI $20bn (+65%); Q1 FY26 AI guided $8.2bn (~2x). "Never seen bookings" like the last three months. Building a Singapore advanced-packaging site; on TSMC 2nm/3nm "so far, we do not have that constraint".
- 2026-03-04: Q1 AI $8.4bn (+106%); Q2 guided $10.7bn (+140%). "Line of sight" to >$100bn AI chip revenue in 2027, with leading-edge wafer/HBM/substrate capacity "fully secured ... for 2026 through 2028". Customer view: Google TPU demand strong; Anthropic 1GW (2026) rising to >3GW (2027); Meta MTIA shipping; OpenAI (sixth customer) >1GW in 2027.
- 2026-06-03: AI bookings >$30bn in the quarter vs $10.8bn shipped (+143% YoY). Q3 AI guided $16bn, FY26 AI $56bn, FY27 >$100bn reiterated. Deals: Google long-term TPU agreement; Anthropic +5GW from 2027; OpenAI 1.3GW committed for 2027; Meta 3GW through 2028 (1GW order in hand); $6bn of POs from two other customers. Apollo/Blackstone AI XPU financing platform for >20GW ($35bn first tranche). Non-AI bookings >$6bn (cyclical recovery); inventory 86 days (from 68) for H2 AI ramp.
Current state (latest call): 2026-09-02 - Q3 AI revenue $16.7bn (more than tripled YoY); FY26 AI raised to $58bn (from $56bn) with Q4 guided $21.7bn (+236%). FY27 ~$115bn on secured supply ("Our demand actually exceeds this outlook"), FY28 $230bn line of sight; Q4 capex $1.4bn for capacity. Six XPU customers (Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta + two others); no digestion signal, and supply is the binding constraint.
