# ASML.AS - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2025-10-15 to 2026-10-14
(3 calls in sample; no calls before Oct-2025 and none for Jan/Apr-2026, so the 2023-24 order slump and the early-2026 order ramp are not covered. Dates are true call dates.)

- 2025-10-15: Q3-25 net sales EUR 7.5bn (including one High-NA system); net bookings EUR 5.4bn, of which EUV EUR 3.6bn. Q4 guide EUR 9.2-9.8bn; FY25 ~EUR 32.5bn at ~52% gross margin.
- 2025-10-15: Tone turns up. "AI is going to benefit a larger part of our customer base", with continued EUV adoption in DRAM and advanced logic. 2026 net sales guided "not below 2025" because China demand is expected to be "significantly lower in 2026" than in 2024-25 (EUV up, DUV down). 2030 revenue opportunity of EUR 44-60bn reiterated.
- 2025-10-15: Named links. ASML led Mistral AI's Series C (~11% stake, board seat); SK hynix is installing its first EXE:5200 High-NA tool in production. Shipped the first XT:260 advanced-packaging scanner (up to 4x productivity).
- 2026-07-15: FY26 guide raised to EUR 43-45bn (gross margin 54-56%); Q2 sales EUR 9.3bn; Q3 guide EUR 11-12bn. Customers revised capex plans upward. Logic system sales grow >25% (3nm for AI accelerators, 2nm ramp) and memory system sales >75%, as DDR/HBM price spikes drive DRAM fab expansion. ~65 Low-NA EUV shipments in 2026 (EUV sales >45% higher).
- 2026-07-15: 2027 Low-NA EUV is close to fully covered by orders, and significant 2028 orders are already in. Low-NA EUV and immersion capacity +30% in 2027, with a further +30% for 2028 under study (85 then 110 EUV units). Backlog rising across a broad customer mix; Installed Base Management >30% growth; China ~20% of 2026 sales; Intel Foundry using High-NA on 18A.
- 2026-10-14: Record Q3 net sales EUR 11.7bn and 57.5% gross margin. FY26 guide EUR 44-45bn (~+36% YoY); Q4 guide EUR 14.2-15.2bn. 65 Low-NA EUV systems in 2026 vs 44 in 2025; memory system sales +75%. Free cash flow EUR 11.4bn on customer down payments.
- 2026-10-14: Shortage regime. Shortages across advanced logic and memory nodes are growing "in size and in time horizon", and customers see demand outpacing supply through at least 2028. ASML is taking 2028 orders and getting requests for 2029-2030 slots. Planned 2027-28 output "will not fully meet the demand", so 2028 capacity growth above +30% is now under study. Price increases agreed on the most critical products (partial effect in 2027, full from 2028).
- 2026-10-14: High-NA adoption broadens: Intel has exposed >1M wafers; Samsung (2028) and TSMC (2030) plan High-NA in high-volume manufacturing. Joint TSMC initiative on larger EUV photomasks; Zeiss is not a bottleneck; ~EUR 500m gain on the Mistral stake.

Current state (latest call): 2026-10-14 - lithography is sold out into 2028, with customers asking for 2029-30 slots. FY26 guide EUR 44-45bn (~+36%), 2027 capacity +30% with more under study for 2028, and price increases from 2027; the binding constraint is now tool and fab capacity, not demand.
