# CIEN - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-03-07 to 2026-09-03
(8 calls in sample; no calls between 2022-09 and 2025-06, so the 2023-24 service-provider inventory digestion and the start of the AI cloud order wave are not covered)

- 2022-03-07: Supply-bound boom. Book-to-bill >2.5, backlog >$3bn; FY22 growth guide of 11-13% maintained despite Q1 revenue of $844.4M being held back by supply; adding production (test) capacity.
- 2022-06-02: FY22 guide cut to mid-single-digit growth only because of component supply. Book-to-bill still well above 1.5 and backlog >$4bn, of which only a few hundred $M is "true 2023 demand"; Q3 guide $870-930M; displacing Huawei in Europe.
- 2022-09-01: Q3 revenue of $868M missed by ~$60M on supplier decommits; Q4 guide cut to $800-880M; orders >30% above revenue, backlog well over $4bn; price increase pushed across customers in early 2022; FY23 growth still guided well above the 6-8% long-term target; India (Jio, Bharti) expected to be robust.
- 2025-06-05: AI inflection now in the numbers. Direct cloud-provider revenue a record >$400M (38% of revenue, +85% YoY); cloud orders on track to double in FY25; awards from three more major cloud providers; FY25 growth raised to ~14%; pluggables to double to >=$150M; largest customer 13.4%, AT&T 10.4%; Q2 revenue $1.13bn, Q3 guide $1.13-1.21bn.
- 2025-12-11: FY25 revenue $4.77bn, record orders $7.8bn, backlog ~$5bn; FY26 guide raised to $5.7-6.1bn (~24% growth vs 17% in September); FY26 capex $250-275M (vs $140M in FY25) for late-2026/2027 demand; lead times extending; scale-across wins at three hyperscalers and Meta DCOM expansion; service-provider orders up ~70%; three customers just under 44% of Q4 revenue (two cloud providers plus AT&T).
- 2026-03-05: "Unprecedented" demand. Record Q1 revenue of $1.43bn; backlog up ~$2bn to ~$7bn, with nearly all new orders for FY27 delivery; FY26 guide raised to $5.9-6.3bn; Q2 guide $1.5bn +/-$50M; cites top-4 hyperscaler 2026 capex above $600bn; Q1 capex $74M including accelerated capacity.
- 2026-06-04: Backlog $7.7bn (+$600M QoQ; ~$6.4bn hardware, ~80% shippable within 12 months); FY26 guide $6.3bn +/-$100M (32% growth); Q3 guide ~$1.625bn; two cloud customers each >10% of revenue; DCOM orders from a second hyperscaler beyond Meta; new hyperscaler win for coherent modules; pluggable revenue to more than double vs 2025; modem (CDM) supply tight.
- 2026-09-03: Q3 revenue $1.67bn (+37%); backlog $8.5bn (+$800M) and expected above $10bn by fiscal year-end, mostly with 2027 request dates and reaching into FY28; cloud revenue +80% and interconnects more than 2x; FY26 midpoint raised to $6.42bn; first FY27 guide of at least $8.3-8.4bn (>=30% growth); component supply locked by LTAs through 2029; capex at the high end of $250-275M.
Current state (latest call): 2026-09-03. Capacity-limited AI optical-networking boom: backlog $8.5bn heading above $10bn, FY27 guided to at least $8.3-8.4bn (+30%), and two cloud customers each above 10% of revenue. Growth is now set by Ciena's own supply and capacity ramp.
