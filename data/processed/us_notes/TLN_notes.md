# TLN — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: only three calls in the corpus (Q3-2025 to Q1-2026).

- 2025-11-05: Q3-25. Executing under the AWS agreements (Susquehanna campus). Hyperscalers are fixated on 2026–2028 power, and Talen expects acceleration "faster than the ramps we put out in June". Cites Amazon's accelerated capacity additions. Reaffirmed 2026 guidance as forwards "tick up"; 2025 tracking at the low end of its range.
- 2026-02-26: Q4-25. The Amazon 2.0 PPA (executed June 2025) moved front-of-the-meter and was upsized to 1.9 GW. Closed the Freedom and Guernsey CCGT acquisitions. Pursuing another ~1 GW data-center contract, with deliveries not before 2028, after an adverse county vote at Montour. "2029 is the new 2028" for data-center power. Reaffirmed 2026 guidance; $2bn buyback.
- 2026-05-05: Q1-26. Reaffirmed 2026. Preliminary FCF/share of ~$34 (2027) and ~$36 (2028), 15% above January estimates (including the pending Cornerstone acquisition). Advancing >2 GW of gas and storage projects at its sites for data-center contracting (500 MW–1 GW per site); ~2 GW submitted to the PJM cluster. Describes a "slow, steady creep up in demand, yet there is still excess capacity", with forward spark spreads rising through 2028.

Current state (latest call, 2026-05-05): One large contract (Amazon 1.9 GW, ramping). The next ~1 GW data-center deal is targeted, with deliveries pushed to 2028+. Outlook is raised on spark spreads and M&A rather than new hyperscaler contracts.
