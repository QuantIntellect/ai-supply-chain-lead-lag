# NOKIA.HE — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: five calls (Q4-2021 to Q2-2022, then Q2-2026 and Q3-2026). Everything from 2022-Q3 to 2026-Q1 is missing.

- 2022-02-03: Q4-21. Net sales €9.7bn (−5% y/y) on lower North America share. Supply-chain constraints are capping growth, and Nokia is building inventory buffers.
- 2022-04-28: Q1-22. Strong order backlog, but supply constraints in mobile and optical cut sales 4% in constant currency. "Demand environment continues to be very strong"; mobile-networks market now seen at +4% (from 3%). Claims pricing power from technology leadership.
- 2022-07-21: Q2-22. Enterprise order intake is strong, with H2 acceleration expected. FY22 sales outlook €23.5–24.7bn, tracking to the higher end.
- 2026-07-23: Q2-26. AI & cloud net sales €446m (+105% y/y) and order intake €2.8bn; Optical Networks +20%. Lead times are elongating on leading-edge supply constraints, and demand is accelerating "higher than what we said at Capital Markets Day". In trials with a US hyperscaler for in-data-center out-of-band management; Google Cloud partnership expanded. Management dismissed double-ordering risk.
- 2026-10-22: Q3-26. AI & cloud net sales +123% y/y (12% of group); orders +118% to €1.5bn gross; IP Networks +43%, Optical +18%. Optical+IP growth is now somewhat above the 18–20% assumption for 2026, with 25–30% expected in 2027; adding €400m of working capital. "The constraint here isn't demand, it's supply." Won a significant deal with a large hyperscaler plus 2 hyperscaler design wins. Partnerships with Google (its €13bn Finland investment) and NVIDIA (AI-RAN).

Current state (latest call, 2026-10-22): AI & cloud is the growth engine (+123%, 12% of sales, €2.8bn + €1.5bn of orders in two quarters). 2027 Optical+IP guide is 25–30%, and supply rather than demand is the binding constraint.
