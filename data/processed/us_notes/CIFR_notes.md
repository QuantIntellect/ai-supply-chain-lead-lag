# CIFR — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only two calls (Q4-25, Q1-26) are in the data.

- 2026-02-24 (Q4-25): two campus leases covering 600 MW gross and ~$9.3bn of contracted revenue (10–15-year terms). During Q4 the Fluidstack lease (backed by Google) at Barber Lake was upsized and a new AWS lease was signed at Black Pearl (~$2bn). Pipeline ~3.4 GW, including the 200 MW Ulysses site (PJM, energizing 2027). Bonds of $2bn at 6.125% and $1.4bn at 7.125% fully fund construction. Demand for power-dense hyperscale infrastructure "continues to outpace supply"; bitcoin mining is winding down.
- 2026-05-05 (Q1-26): third campus lease signed, with an investment-grade hyperscale tenant. Contracted revenue $11.4bn; 907 MW operating plus contracted. 3.3 GW pipeline with Reveille and Ulysses energizing in 2027. $200m revolver. Building fast gives "more pricing power".

Current state (latest call): $11.4bn contracted with three hyperscale-backed leases (Fluidstack/Google, AWS, plus an unnamed investment-grade tenant) and a 3.3 GW pipeline.
