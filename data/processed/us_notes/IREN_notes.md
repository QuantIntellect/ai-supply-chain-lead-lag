# IREN — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only three calls (FQ2–FQ4 FY26) are in the data.

- 2026-02-05 (Q2-FY26): $9.7bn AI cloud contract with Microsoft; $3.6bn of GPU financing at under 6% plus prepayments cover ~95% of the related GPU capex. $2.3bn of run-rate revenue (ARR) under contract. End-2026 target: $3.4bn ARR on 140k GPUs, using only ~10% of 4.5 GW of secured power. The constraint is construction pace, not power or capital.
- 2026-05-07 (Q3-FY26; file dated 2026-05-08): contracted ARR $3.1bn; exit-2026 target raised to $3.7bn (150k GPUs). Signed a $3.54bn five-year AI cloud contract with NVIDIA (60 MW at Childress, ~$700m ARR), and NVIDIA's $2.1bn investment vests as GPUs are deployed. Secured power raised to 5 GW, with new sites in Europe (Nostrum, 490 MW in Spain) and APAC. 480 MW of AI capacity to be delivered in 2026; demand "certainly outstripping supply".
- 2026-08-27 (Q4-FY26; file dated 2026-08-31): $4bn of ARR contracted for 2026 capacity, $1bn of it operating. New multiyear contracts with Cohere, Prometheus, Perplexity, Figure AI, Fal AI, Higgsfield AI and an undisclosed "leading frontier AI lab". FY27 capex $25–30bn. $6.5bn of GPU financing raised in three months; prepayments now fund 45–55% of GPU capex. Late-stage talks for a significant part of 2027 capacity; more than 5 GW announced. Data-center and GPU cost inflation of ~15–20%.

Current state (latest call): contracted ARR stepped up from $2.3bn to $4bn in two quarters. FY27 capex is $25–30bn. Customers are prepaying about half of GPU capex, a sign that demand exceeds supply.
