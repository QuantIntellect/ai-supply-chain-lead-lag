# DLR — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-02-17: FY21 bookings $500m (+15%), with Q4 at $156m and records in both the 0–1MW and >1MW segments. Inventory is tightening. 2022 cash renewal spreads guided ~flat (vs slightly negative in 2021), and capex steps up to $2.3–2.5bn. Graphcore (AI accelerators) selected PlatformDIGITAL.
- 2022-04-28: Record bookings of $167m, led by >1MW. The development pipeline hit a record 44 projects and >300MW, 58% pre-sold. "When demand exceeds supply, we've been able to raise our prices." Hyperscale demand is not slowing.
- 2022-07-28: Dominion warned of an Eastern Loudoun (Northern Virginia) power "pinch point" that could delay deliveries until 2026. Bookings $113m. Pipeline >360MW under construction, with 100MW+ of new starts. Price increases are now producing positive cash renewals.
- 2022-10-26: Record $176m bookings, but the "sales cycle lengthen[s] and global uncertainty extends decision times" (small enterprises softer). Reduced power availability (Loudoun) is constraining new supply, and DLR is pushing prices higher.
- 2023-02-16: Record backlog of $477m, while Q4-22 bookings moderated to $117m. 2023 cash re-leasing spreads guided >3%. First AI framing: ChatGPT in Bing and Google's Bard signal "the broader introduction of AI" and a future wave of compute demand.
- 2023-04-27 to 2023-07-27: Q1-23 bookings dipped to $83m, with Northern Virginia leasing at $2.5m vs ~$20m a quarter in 2H22 because of power limits; management called it timing, saying hyperscale demand "remains really healthy". Renewal spreads rose to +4.5%, then +6.9% (strongest in three years). 8MW in Ashburn was recaptured and re-leased "at a substantial premium". AI is now showing up in both the hyperscale and 0–1MW pipelines.
- 2023-10-26: 2023 development-capex guide raised $400m (at the midpoint) to $2.7–2.9bn on leasing and pipeline demand. DLR is supporting a customer deployment of 32,000 NVIDIA H100s. >1MW pricing is the highest since 1H16. New supply is constrained by power, supply chains and financing.
- 2024-02-15: "2023 will be revered as the year of AI's arrival to the data center forefront." Signed a $7bn development JV with Blackstone (~500MW). 2024 gross capex >$3.5bn (~+15% y/y). Power and supply-chain constraints keep "the pricing pendulum in our favor".
- 2024-05-02: Record $252m bookings (prior record $176m), ~50% of them AI. Leased ~80MW in power-constrained Eastern Loudoun. Record cash renewal spread of +11.8% (3.4% normalized).
- 2024-07-25 to 2024-10-24: Q2-24 bookings fell to $164m and the backlog eased to $527m (−2% q/q) in "the most supply constrained moment in the last 20 years". Q3-24 then set records: bookings $521m ($450m >1MW, ~50% AI per management Q&A), backlog $859m (+60% q/q), cash renewal spread +15.2%. The pipeline grew ~50% to 644MW under construction.
- 2025-02-13: Q4-24 bookings $100m; backlog ~$800m, down from the $859m record. Management noted the hyperscale "spigot of demand is not running at full" around the clock. Pipeline $7bn+, 70% pre-leased (644MW). 2025 capex guided $3.0–3.5bn net / ~$4.5bn gross. The data has no Q1-25 call.
- 2025-07-24 to 2025-10-23: Record 0–1MW plus interconnection bookings ($90m in Q2-25; total $177m gross / $135m at DLR share). Renewal spreads +7.3% and +8%. Backlog $852m. Record AI bookings within the enterprise (0–1MW) segment. Land-to-shell runway of 5GW. Management: "we're gonna be spending more in 2026."
- 2026-02-05: FY25 bookings >$1bn (100% share) for the second straight year; record backlog ~$1.4bn (100% share); record 0–1MW bookings ~$340m (+35%); FY25 renewal spreads 6.7%. 2026 capex guided $3.25–3.75bn net, and the first hyperscale fund drew >$3.2bn of LP equity. Management expects demand to grow 2.5–3x over five years while supply is "severely constrained".
- 2026-04-23: Signed >$700m of leases (100% share; $423m at DLR share), including the largest lease in DLR's history (its first hyperscale deployment in Charlotte). Record backlog $1.8bn (100%) / $1.0bn (DLR share); AI >20% of bookings. 2026 capex raised $250m to $3.5–4.0bn, renewal-spread guide raised to 6.5–8.5% (+50bp), and FFO guidance raised. The data has no Q2-26 call.

Current state (latest call 2026-04-23): accelerating. Record signings and record backlog, with hyperscale/AI blocks in demand across 2026–2028. Capex guidance is rising (2026 at $3.5–4.0bn net), and renewal pricing is firming because supply is limited by power, zoning and supply chains.
