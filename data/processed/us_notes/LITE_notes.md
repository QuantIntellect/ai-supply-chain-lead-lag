# LITE - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-03 to 2026-08-11
(7 calls in sample; no calls between 2022-08 and 2025-08, so the 2023-24 telecom/datacom downturn and the first AI ramp are not covered. Fiscal year ends late June.)

- 2022-02-03: Demand accelerating but held back by supply. Third-party IC shortages left a $50M supply-demand gap in FQ2, expected to exceed $65M in FQ3. Extra EML capacity was coming online in March for datacom, and more had been ordered for late CY22-CY23 because "we're still not going to be caught up with demand".
- 2022-05-04: The gap widened to >$100M of FQ4 revenue as demand accelerated across telecom, datacom and lasers. Management expected customer demand to outpace third-party supply into CY2023; new EML capacity was ramping datacom shipments.
- 2022-08-16: Telecom/datacom revenue +16% QoQ, but demand still exceeded supply by ~$100M (seen shrinking to ~$75M in FQ1). EML capacity nearly doubled in FY22. FY23 revenue was guided up >25% (incl. NeoPhotonics), but the largest (3D-sensing) customer was shrinking to 10-15% of revenue as its demand dipped "much faster than we expected".
- 2025-08-12: The AI cloud ramp (first call after the gap). FQ4 revenue of $480.7M beat the raised guide; cloud & networking revenue grew +16% QoQ and +67% YoY on hyperscale demand. Cloud transceiver modules were shipping to three hyperscalers. The $600M quarterly revenue target was pulled in to the June-2026 quarter or earlier. FQ1 guide $510-540M. Management cited a "major supply constraint".
- 2026-02-03: Record FQ2 revenue of $665.5M and an FQ3 guide of $780-830M (~85% YoY). OCS backlog "surged well past $400 million", mostly shipping in 2H CY26, and another multi-$100M order for CPO ultra-high-power lasers was secured. It was undershipping customer demand by ~30% even after adding ~20% capacity. Capex $84M.
- 2026-05-05: Record revenue of $808M (+90% YoY); FQ4 guide $960M-1.01bn. The supply-demand gap was ">30%", and EML supply was set to rise 50% from Dec 2025 to Dec 2026. NVIDIA's direct investment drove a $2.02bn cash increase. A multiyear, multibillion-dollar OCS purchase agreement was announced, and Google is "driving a lot of demand" as one of the largest customers. Customers were asked to co-fund capex through prepayment, take-or-pay or price increases.
- 2026-08-11: FQ4 revenue $1.01bn (+109% YoY). FQ1 FY27 guide of $1.225-1.275bn, whose midpoint hits the $1.25bn target more than a quarter early on a "sharp acceleration in AI revenue". The first triple-digit-million OCS quarter is guided, and pump-laser shipments are to rise fourfold. "The demand signals increased and we are very much further behind" supply. It is adding contract-manufacturer capacity for a 2027 whose demand signal is "incredibly strong". Capex $167M.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-11 - an FQ1 FY27 guide of $1.225-1.275bn after FQ4 revenue of $1.01bn (+109% YoY). Demand for EML/CW/pump lasers, 1.6T transceivers and OCS keeps outrunning supply (the gap was >30% in May and has widened since). NVIDIA is now an investor and Google a top customer.
