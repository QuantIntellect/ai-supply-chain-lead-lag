# COHR - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-09 to 2026-08-12
(17 calls in sample; no calls between 2025-02 and 2025-11, so fiscal Q3-Q4 FY25 are missing. II-VI until it bought Coherent in 2022; fiscal year ends June.)

- 2022-02-09: (II-VI) Demand "lit up across the board". Fiscal Q2 revenue of $807M would have cleared the top of guidance without supply-chain constraints. FY22 capex was set at $325-375M for SiC and InP expansion. An early AI framing: hyperscalers building "AI superclusters" in a datacom market that analysts expected to grow 20% in CY22.
- 2022-05-10: Record $1.2bn of new orders and backlog of $2.1bn (+88% YoY). Shortages cost $65M of FQ3 revenue, and the gap was guided to widen to ~$100M in FQ4 as demand rose "even faster than our increased rate of supply". FY22 capex was raised to at or above the top of the $325-375M range. FQ4 guide $840-880M.
- 2022-11-09: Constraints still held back $64M (it would have shipped >$1.4bn). First FY23 guide of $5.25-5.55bn, with visibility of very strong demand through 1H CY23. First hint of a hyperscaler slowdown ("some hyperscalers ... decide to slow down"); management said its customer mix was not affected.
- 2023-02-08: The FY23 guide was nudged up to $5.35-5.55bn and backlog stood at $2.9bn. But customers were "returning to more normal patterns of ordering and inventory management": order horizons were shrinking from ~12 to ~6 months. Supply constraints still cost $67M, and 800G deployments for AI/ML were accelerating.
- 2023-05-10: Demand broke. FQ3 revenue of $1.24bn came in 6% below the low end of guidance, and the FY23 outlook was cut to $5.08-5.15bn. Hyperscale/enterprise datacom and telecom customers "abruptly shifted" from managing shortages to managing inventory surpluses, and display customers cut their outlook by >35%. In the same call, AI/800G deployments were "heating up in terms of planning".
- 2023-08-16: A "reset year": FY24 revenue guide of $4.5-4.7bn (FQ1 $1-1.1bn) as customers cut legacy orders and inventory, and a ~10% datacom customer's legacy demand was rolling off. Partly offset by a "surge in orders" for AI in FQ4 and a guide that "contemplates a meaningful amount" of 800G revenue. Hyperscalers were to be ~two-thirds of FY24 datacom revenue. Backlog $2.7bn.
- 2023-11-07: A second straight quarter of "extremely strong" demand for AI transceivers, plus engagement with "new significant AI players". Telecom suffered both end-market softness and inventory digestion at customers. FY24 guide held at $4.5-4.7bn. 80% of FY25 datacom transceiver revenue was expected to come from hyperscalers. Mitsubishi Electric and Denso committed $1bn to the SiC business.
- 2024-02-06: AI shows up in the numbers: >$100M of 800G transceivers shipped in the December quarter, up almost 100% QoQ, and 50% of FY24 datacom transceiver revenue expected to be AI-related. Demand was "definitely stronger than what we can supply". FY24 guide raised to $4.55-4.70bn.
- 2024-05-07: First signs of an AI air pocket. FQ3 book-to-bill was below 1, and 800G order lead times were shrinking from close to a year. Management still saw "strong growth in overall 800G and AI-related demand", and the FY24 guide was raised again to $4.62-4.7bn.
- 2024-11-06: Datacom revenue +16% QoQ and +89% YoY, and networking +12% QoQ and +61% YoY, both on AI data-center demand. 1.6T samples were shipped, with a revenue ramp expected in CY2025. FQ2 guide $1.33-1.41bn. Telecom recovery was still viewed cautiously.
- 2025-11-05: Growth was now gated by supply. Revenue was $1.58bn; data center grew +4% QoQ and +23% YoY, "constrained by indium phosphide laser supply", and was guided to accelerate to ~10% QoQ. Orders for 800G and 1.6T were "extremely strong", and large customers gave forecasts out to 2028. Internal InP capacity was to roughly double over the next year on 6-inch lines, plus a Penang expansion. FQ2 guide $1.56-1.7bn.
- 2026-02-04: Data-center book-to-bill above 4x, and data-center revenue +14% QoQ (+36% YoY). CY26 was mostly booked and new bookings were landing in CY27. Capex was $154M (from $104M) and rising each quarter, and pricing was being optimized. Revenue $1.69bn; FQ3 guide $1.7-1.84bn.
- 2026-05-06: Record bookings and backlog, with orders into CY2028 and LTAs to the end of the decade. NVIDIA partnership: a $2bn equity investment plus a multiyear CPO laser supply agreement. InP is "the key constraint": capacity to double by next quarter and more than double again by end-2027. Capex $290M. Revenue $1.8bn; FQ4 guide $1.91-2.05bn.
- 2026-08-12: Record FQ4 revenue of $2.05bn (+13% QoQ, +34% YoY); FY26 revenue $7.12bn. FQ1 FY27 guide of $2.2-2.4bn, with data-center growth >80% YoY. FY27 is "basically completely booked out" through CY2027. FQ4 capex was $556M and is rising again. InP is still the primary constraint, and more capacity is planned beyond 2027.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-12 - record FQ4 FY26 revenue of $2.05bn and an FQ1 FY27 guide of $2.2-2.4bn (data center >80% YoY). FY27 is essentially booked out through CY2027, with orders into 2028 and LTAs (incl. NVIDIA's) to the end of the decade. Growth is gated by indium-phosphide laser supply, not demand.
