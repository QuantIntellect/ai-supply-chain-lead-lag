# KLAC - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-01-27 to 2026-04-29
(16 calls in sample; no 2023-04 or 2025-04 calls, and the July-2026 call is not in the sample)

- 2022-01-27: Supply-bound peak. "Record backlog"; CY22 WFE seen growing high teens to top $100bn (from ~$86bn in 2021); KLA revenue growth guided above 20%; Mar-Q guide $2.2bn +/-$100M framed as the 2022 low point because supply constraints and lead times cap output; slots being booked into 2023.
- 2022-07-28: First cracks. Customers flag PC/mobile softening and weaker memory pricing; memory capex adjustments; CY22 WFE cut to ~$95bn (high-single-digit growth, from >$100bn) on supply limits; KLA still sees demand above supply in 2H22; Sep-Q guide $2.6bn; ~25% of process-control system shipments go to China.
- 2022-10-26: Downturn call. Plans on CY23 WFE down ~20%, memory down more; new US China export rules cut CY23 revenue by an estimated $600-900M gross (~$100M in the Dec-Q guide of $2.8bn +/-$150M).
- 2023-01-26: CY22 WFE ~$94-95bn; CY23 still ~-20%, DRAM down more than NAND; inventory reserves taken as the outlook "weakened at an accelerated pace"; Mar-Q guide $2.35bn +/-$150M; leading-edge products still supply-constrained.
- 2023-07-27: Trough. Memory WFE ~-40% in 2023; demand "stabilizing"; first AI pull: accelerated orders for advanced-packaging work tied to AI accelerators; Sep-Q guide $2.35bn.
- 2023-10-25: Demand stabilizing at current levels, recovery timing unclear; China fills the gap (more than $800M of deposits from these customers); Dec-Q guide $2.45bn.
- 2024-04-25: The turn. Customers now ask about tool availability and slots ("conversations we haven't had for a while"); N2 orders expected late 2024 for 2025; advanced packaging ~$400M run-rate in 2024; export controls cost ~10% of the China market; some lead times 18-24 months; Jun-Q guide $2.5bn.
- 2024-07-24: "Transitioning from stabilization to resumption of growth" (from the June quarter); AI driving leading-edge acceleration (N3/N2, packaging, HBM), but HBM capacity adds seen only in 2025-26; advanced-packaging estimate raised to >$500M (from $400M); CY24 WFE mid-$90bn; book-to-bill just below 1; Sep-Q guide $2.75bn.
- 2024-10-30: Leading-edge customers ask for acceleration "for first time in quite a long time"; CY24 WFE raised to the high-$90bn range; China expected to fall to ~30% of revenue in 2025 as it digests; Dec-Q guide $2.95bn.
- 2025-01-30: Mar-Q guide $3bn +/-$150M (+27% YoY); CY25 WFE mid-single-digit growth; advanced packaging >$800M (raised from $750M); says AI compute-efficiency gains will not hurt demand; China export-control hit ~$500M +/-$100M.
- 2025-07-31: June-Q revenue +24% YoY on leading-edge logic, HBM and advanced packaging; packaging >$925M (from $850M); "no material changes" in customer demand; RPO ~$7.9bn as lead times normalize; Sep-Q guide $3.15bn.
- 2025-10-29: CY25 WFE mid-to-high single digits (modestly raised); memory (mostly DRAM, 78% of memory) rises to 41% of systems mix; 2026 seen as roughly flat 1H, accelerating 2H; Dec-Q guide $3.225bn; packaging >$925M (+~70%).
- 2026-01-29: Supply-constrained upturn. "Virtually sold out" for 1H26 and lead times extending (optics the longest pole); CY26 core WFE low-$120bn (from ~$110bn) plus ~$12bn packaging; new orders are for late-2026/2027 delivery; DRAM cost inflation trims gross margin 75-100bp; Mar-Q guide $3.35bn; 2025 packaging systems revenue $950M (+70%).
- 2026-04-29: Raised everything. CY26 WFE including packaging >$140bn; KLA 2026 growth "high teen", process-control systems >20%; 2027 growth expected to exceed 2026 with "unprecedented demand visibility"; customers constrained by fab space; packaging process-control revenue ~$1bn (from ~$635M); Jun-Q guide $3.575bn +/-$200M.
Current state (latest call): 2026-04-29 (latest in sample). Supply-constrained upcycle: 2026 WFE >$140bn, KLA systems growth >20%, June-Q guide $3.575bn, and 2027 growth guided above 2026 on greenfield fab projects. Constraints are clean-room space and long-lead optics, not demand.
