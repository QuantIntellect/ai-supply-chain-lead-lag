# CSCO - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-16 to 2026-08-12
(17 calls in sample; fiscal year ends late July; no calls between 2025-02-12 and 2025-11-12, so the May/Aug-2025 calls are missing)

- 2022-02-16: Supply-constrained order boom: product orders +33% (third straight quarter at or above 30%), enterprise +37%, web-scale orders +70% YoY. Product backlog above $14bn (+150% YoY), software backlog above $2bn; demand "continues to outpace supply"; FY22 revenue growth guide 5.5-6.5%; price increases still flowing through backlog.
- 2022-05-18: First crack: orders slowed to +8% (enterprise flat) even as backlog hit a record of well over $15bn (+130% YoY). Shanghai lockdowns hit power-supply parts; Q4 guided to -1% to -5.5% and the FY22 growth guide cut to 2-3%.
- 2022-08-17: FY22 orders +14% with record backlog and RPO above $31bn; Q4 orders -6% against a +31% comp. Web-scale orders +50% on a trailing 4-quarter basis, and the first 800G systems shipped to a web-scale customer. The FY23 guide of 4-6% is "absolutely supply constrained".
- 2023-02-15: Supply easing turns backlog into revenue: FY23 guide raised to 9-10.5% (the second raise, after 2022-11-16). Orders -22% YoY as lead times normalize; the two largest web-scale customers grew orders more than 40% in H1 FY23; year-end backlog expected at ~2x normal.
- 2023-05-17: Digestion begins: orders -23% as customers absorb shipments, and weighted lead times are down 40% over two quarters. FY23 guide 10-10.5% (Q4 +14-16%); AI infrastructure market sized at $8.5bn by 2027.
- 2023-08-16: First AI number: over half a billion dollars of AI Ethernet fabric orders to date. But hyperscalers are "obviously in digest mode", with orders resuming late in calendar 2023. Q4 orders +30% QoQ (-14% YoY); FY24 guide $57-58.2bn; excess backlog (~2x historical) ships mostly in Q1 FY24.
- 2023-11-15: Guide cut: FY24 revenue lowered to $53.8-55bn as Q1 orders fell 20% and customers install 1-2 quarters of shipped inventory. AI: more than $500M of orders taken and line of sight to over $1bn of AI orders from major cloud providers in FY25; 3 of the top 4 hyperscalers deploy its Ethernet AI fabric.
- 2024-02-14: Second cut, to $51.5-52.5bn. Digestion is taking "longer than our initial expectations": cloud providers hold 20+ weeks of inventory, Q2 orders -12%, telco/cable weak. Backlog is back to normal.
- 2024-08-14: Turn: Q4 orders +14% (+6% ex-Splunk), with digestion "largely behind us". Cumulative AI orders from web-scale passed $1bn, and another $1bn of AI orders is expected in FY25; FY25 guide $55-56.2bn.
- 2024-11-13: Orders +20% (+9% organic), web-scale orders more than doubled, and service provider & cloud orders +28%. AI orders above $300M in Q1, on track to beat the $1bn FY25 target; FY25 guide raised to $55.3-56.3bn; hyperscalers deploy Cisco 8000 with Silicon One G200 in back-end AI networks.
- 2025-11-12: AI step-up: AI infrastructure orders $1.3bn in Q1 FY26, with ~$3bn of FY26 hyperscale AI revenue and at least 2x FY25 AI orders expected. Service provider & cloud orders +45%; sovereign/neocloud/enterprise AI pipeline above $2bn; inventory plus purchase commitments up almost $1bn QoQ (+38% YoY); FY26 guide $60.2-61bn; G42 and NVIDIA (N9100 on Spectrum-X) partnerships.
- 2026-02-11: Hyperscale AI orders hit $2.1bn in Q2, equal to all of FY25; the FY26 AI orders outlook was raised to above $5bn (AI revenue above $3bn), and the FY26 revenue guide to $61.2-61.7bn. Memory inflation is driving price increases; advance purchase commitments +$1.8bn in 90 days (+73% YoY). Neocloud/sovereign AI orders $350M (pipeline above $2.5bn); 1 GW JV with AMD and HUMAIN by 2030.
- 2026-05-13: FY26 hyperscale AI orders raised to ~$9bn (YTD $5.3bn already above the prior $5bn); FY26 AI revenue ~$4bn and FY27 at least $6bn. Orders +35%, service provider & cloud +105% (top-5 hyperscalers each at triple digits); 5 new hyperscaler design wins; Acacia orders above $1bn; FY26 guide $62.8-63bn.
- 2026-08-12: FY26 hyperscale AI orders reached $9.3bn (~4.5x FY25) against ~$4bn of AI revenue, so it enters FY27 with a large backlog. FY27 hyperscale AI revenue target $7.5bn; FY27 revenue guide $72.2-73.4bn (~10% growth ex-AI); orders +35%, service provider & cloud +95%. Supply described as "adequate", with direct TSMC sourcing and no allocation wait.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-12: strongest demand in the sample. FY26 hyperscale AI orders were $9.3bn vs ~$4bn of AI revenue; guides FY27 AI revenue of $7.5bn within a $72.2-73.4bn FY27 revenue guide. Product orders +35% (hyperscale triple-digit, enterprise +21%), and management says supply is adequate even if demand exceeds the guide.
