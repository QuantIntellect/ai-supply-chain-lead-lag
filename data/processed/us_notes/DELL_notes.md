# DELL - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2025-11-25 to 2026-09-01
(4 calls in sample, Q3 FY26 to Q2 FY27; no calls from 2022 to mid-2025)

- 2025-11-25: Q3 FY26 AI orders $12.3bn (record; $30bn YTD), AI server shipments $5.6bn, record AI backlog $18.4bn shifting toward GB300. The five-quarter pipeline is "multiples of our backlog" across neoclouds, sovereigns and enterprise.
- 2025-11-25: Raised FY26 AI server shipments to ~$25bn (+150%; the analyst noted "from 20"), with ~$9.4bn in Q4; Q4 revenue guide $31-32bn; FY26 revenue ~$111.7bn.
- 2025-11-25: Early memory cost shock: "We have not seen costs move at the rate that we've seen"; the cost basis is rising across all products (DRAM and beyond), and Dell is repricing to recoup it.
- 2026-02-26: Q4 FY26 AI orders $34.1bn; FY26 AI orders $64.1bn vs $25.2bn shipped; record AI backlog $43bn, "overwhelmingly" Grace Blackwell. Vera Rubin appears only in the 5-quarter pipeline; AI customers >4,000.
- 2026-02-26: FY27 guide: revenue $138-142bn and AI revenue ~$50bn (~+100%). In traditional servers "demand significantly outpaced supply". Shorter quote validity and dynamic pricing pass memory costs through.
- 2026-05-28: Q1 FY27 AI orders $24.4bn, AI server revenue $16.1bn, AI backlog $51.3bn; total revenue $43.8bn (+88%); >5,000 AI customers.
- 2026-05-28: Raised FY27 revenue to $165-169bn and AI server revenue to ~$60bn (+$10bn). "Demand continues to exceed supply, with memory as the primary constraint"; memory uncertainty is driving customers to secure supply over longer periods (pull-forward).
- 2026-09-01: Q2 FY27 record AI orders $60.9bn, AI server revenue $16.4bn, record AI backlog $95bn ($131.7bn of orders over 12 months). Traditional server and networking revenue $10.5bn (+122%) as demand outpaced supply; >6,500 AI customers.
- 2026-09-01: Raised the FY27 revenue guide by $25bn to a $192bn midpoint (+~70%), with AI server revenue ~$74bn (3x) and EPS $25.50. First to ship NVIDIA Vera Rubin rack systems. Constraints: "DRAM, DRAM, DRAM, followed by NAND", plus leading-node and mature-node parts (MOSFETs, power ICs).
Current state (latest call): 2026-09-01. AI orders are accelerating ($60.9bn in Q2, backlog $95bn), and the FY27 guide is raised to $192bn with AI servers at ~$74bn. Supply (DRAM, then NAND) is the limiter, not demand, and demand is broadening into enterprise traditional servers.
