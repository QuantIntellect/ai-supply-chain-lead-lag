# CRDO - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-06-01 to 2026-09-01
(7 calls in sample: no calls between 2022-09 and 2025-06, and none for 2025-12, so the FY24 slowdown and the start of the 2024-25 AEC ramp are not covered)

- 2022-06-01: Record Q4-FY22 revenue $37.5M (+90% YoY); FY22 $106.5M (+81%). A COVID lockdown at a China supply partner meant "we were simply not able to fulfill all demand". Firm backlog runs 52 weeks out at many large customers. The first large AEC customer (an unnamed hyperscaler) was 30% of FY22 revenue. FY23 outlook at least $200M (>88% growth); Q1 guide $43.5-47.5M (+324% YoY).
- 2022-06-01: AEC pipeline: a third major program at the first hyperscaler and a ramp at a second hyperscaler, both in FY23. Customers are asking for 52-week demand commitments because of supply-chain tightness.
- 2022-08-31: Record Q1-FY23 revenue $46.5M (+24% QoQ). Supply fully recovered and buffer inventory built ($37M, +$9.7M). Production ramping at a second major data-center customer (400G MACsec AEC); the first customer gives a 12-month outlook. Q2 guide $48.5-52.5M (+91% YoY); FY23 of at least $200M reiterated. TSMC wafer planning shows no issues; Tesla named Credo its key connectivity partner for Dojo.
- 2025-06-02: AI inflection (first call after the gap): Q4-FY25 revenue $170M (+26% QoQ, +180% YoY); FY25 $436.8M (+126%). Very concentrated: the largest customer was 61% of revenue, plus 12% and 11% customers. xAI named as a customer. FY26 outlook above $800M (>85%); Q1 guide $185-195M. Two more hyperscalers ramp in 2H FY26, and capex may double.
- 2025-09-03: Q1-FY26 revenue $223.1M (+31% QoQ, +274% YoY). FY26 outlook raised to about 120% growth (vs >85% guided on 2025-06-02); Q2 guide $230-240M. Customers at 35%/33%/20%. The fourth hyperscaler is ramping and expected to exceed 10% of FY26 revenue. Optical revenue on track to double again.
- 2026-03-02: Record Q3-FY26 revenue $407M (+52% QoQ, >200% YoY), at the high end of revised guidance, with "significant upside demand". Top three customers at 39%/32%/17%. Q4 guide $425-435M. First FY27 view: growth above 50%. Zero-Flap optics ahead of schedule, with production shipments to neocloud TensorWave. Memory has "all of a sudden" become an industry concern; Credo says its wafer (12/7/5/3nm) and packaging supply is secured.
- 2026-06-01: FY26 revenue $1.3bn (+206%); Q4 $437M (+7% QoQ). FY27 outlook raised to >80% growth (from >50%), driven by more than $600M of optical revenue, with DSPs, PICs and ZF optics each above $100M. Q1-FY27 guide $465-475M. "There is a significant tightness in the supply chain", but capacity commitments are in place for a "very aggressive" 2H ramp. Four 10% customers (34/27/16/10%). Neoclouds and tier-2 clouds could reach about 20% of revenue.
- 2026-09-01: Q1-FY27 revenue $479M (+10% QoQ, +115% YoY), the seventh straight quarter of triple-digit growth. FY27 outlook raised again to >85%; Q2 guide $525-535M; optical above $600M on track. Deep relationships with 5 hyperscalers; top customers 33/28/13/10%. Inventory $313.1M (+$62.2M) built ahead of 2H ramps. Microsoft named as the first AEC customer.
Current state (latest call): 2026-09-01 - accelerating: Q1-FY27 revenue $479M (+115% YoY), Q2 guide $525-535M, and the FY27 outlook raised for the second time in two quarters (>50%, then >80%, then >85%) on a 2H optical ramp above $600M. Supply is tight industry-wide but committed. Concentration is falling (top customer 61%, then 33%) as the 4th and 5th hyperscalers and neoclouds ramp.
