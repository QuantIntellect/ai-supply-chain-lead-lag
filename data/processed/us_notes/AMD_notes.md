# AMD - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-01 to 2026-08-04
(17 calls in sample; the 2025-05 (Q1-25) and 2025-10/11 (Q3-25) calls are missing, so the OpenAI deal announcement quarter is seen only in later calls)

- 2022-02-01: Record 2021 revenue of $16.4bn (+68%), with data center more than doubling to a mid-20s % share of revenue. FY22 guide about $21.5bn (+31%). AMD invested about $1bn in long-term supply capacity in 2021 and says supply is tight. Data-center GPU (MI200) revenue more than doubled on HPC supercomputer wins.
- 2022-05-03: FY22 growth outlook raised to about 60% (about $26.3bn including Xilinx) from about 31%; organic growth in the mid-30s. Q2 guide about $6.5bn (+69% YoY). Server revenue has more than doubled YoY in 8 of the last 10 quarters. Wafers at 16nm and above are still constrained; TSMC is the primary wafer supplier.
- 2022-11-01: Downturn: Q3 revenue of $5.6bn came in below expectations on a PC supply-chain inventory correction (Client -40% YoY). FY22 guide cut to about $23.5bn (about $26.3bn had been guided on 2022-08-02). Cloud revenue still more than doubled and server set its 10th straight record quarter, but management flagged near-term "optimization" of footprints at individual cloud vendors.
- 2023-01-31: Cloud digestion reaches data center: Q1-23 data-center revenue is "expected to decline due to elevated levels of inventory with some cloud customers". Q1 guide about $5.3bn (-10% YoY); Client $903M (-51% YoY). Data center plus embedded grew from $3.9bn (2021) to $10.6bn (2022). MI300 previewed for El Capitan (Lawrence Livermore).
- 2023-05-02: Trough guide: Q2 about $5.3bn (-19% YoY); data center $1.3bn, flat YoY. MI300 interest "increased significantly", with revenue from cloud AI customers starting in Q4-23 and becoming "more meaningful in 2024".
- 2023-08-01: AI demand shows up in engagement: AI cluster engagements up more than 7x QoQ, and the AI-accelerator market is seen above $150bn by 2027. Core data center still weak: $1.3bn (-11% YoY) on soft enterprise demand and elevated customer inventory. Embedded to decline in 2H as lead times normalize. Q3 guide about $5.7bn (+2.5% YoY).
- 2023-10-31: First AI revenue target: data-center GPU revenue of about $400M in Q4-23 and above $2bn in 2024, backed by "purchase commitments from cloud customers". Multiple large hyperscalers committed to MI300. Q4 guide about $6.1bn (+9% YoY). Embedded inventory digestion runs through 1H24.
- 2024-01-30: Record data-center revenue of $2.3bn (+38% YoY, +43% QoQ), with Q4 data-center GPU above $400M. 2024 data-center GPU outlook raised to above $3.5bn (from above $2bn), with supply secured to ship "substantially more". AI-accelerator market seen at about $400bn in 2027. Microsoft, Oracle and Meta are deploying Instinct.
- 2024-10-29: Fourth raise of 2024: data-center GPU above $5bn (above $4.5bn in July, $2bn at the start of the year). Data center a record $3.5bn (+122% YoY). AI-accelerator market seen growing more than 60% a year to $500bn in 2028. "Tight supply environment", but capacity is secured. Q4 guide about $7.5bn (+22% YoY). Meta and Microsoft are scaling MI300X.
- 2025-02-04: More than $5bn of data-center AI revenue in 2024; data center a record $3.9bn (+69% YoY). Air pocket: 1H25 data-center GPU roughly flat vs 2H24 ahead of MI350, whose production is pulled in to mid-2025. Q1 guide about $7.1bn (+30% YoY).
- 2025-08-05: Export-control shock: about $800M MI308 inventory charge; data center $3.2bn (+14% YoY, -12% QoQ). The Q3 guide of about $8.7bn excludes any MI308 China revenue. Oracle is building a 27,000+ node MI355X cluster, there are more than 40 sovereign engagements, and Sanmina becomes the AI rack-manufacturing partner.
- 2026-02-03: Data center a record $5.4bn (+39% YoY), including about $390M of MI308 China revenue. Q1 guide about $9.8bn (+32% YoY). Six-gigawatt multi-generation OpenAI deployment; "tens of billions" of data-center AI revenue in 2027; data center to grow above 60% a year over 3-5 years. Server CPU supply capacity raised amid tightness.
- 2026-05-05: Meta signs for up to 6GW of Instinct, including a custom MI450. MI450/Helios customer forecasts are above the initial 2027 plan. Server-CPU market growth outlook raised to above 35% a year (above $120bn by 2030) from about 18%. Data center $5.8bn (+57% YoY); Q2 guide about $11.2bn (+46% YoY). Supply chain "tight"; higher memory costs will cut 2H26 PC and gaming demand.
- 2026-08-04: Data center a record $6.7bn (+107% YoY), 58% of revenue. Anthropic will deploy up to 2GW of MI450/Helios from 1H27, and Microsoft will deploy Helios on Azure. Server growth above 80% in 2H26 and above 70% in 2027. Data center and data-center AI growth "well over 100%" in 2027. Q3 guide about $13bn (+41% YoY). HBM allocation is visible for 2027.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-04 - accelerating: data center +107% YoY to $6.7bn and a Q3 guide of about $13bn (+41% YoY). Gigawatt-scale "strategic anchor customers" (OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic at up to 2GW) start ramping with Helios in late 2026 and 2027, and data-center growth "well over 100%" is expected in 2027. Constraints are supply-chain and memory tightness (HBM allocation secured; PC and gaming hit by memory costs).
