# HUBB — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: calls for Q4-21 to Q2-22 and Q2-25 to Q1-26 only. The Q2-2025 digest section is empty, so it was read from the transcript.

- 2022-02-03 (Q4-21): 2021 orders up more than 30% with a record backlog. Output to stay supply-constrained into 2022; adding T&D components capacity.
- 2022-04-26 (Q1-22): orders +25% vs shipments +21%, so backlog grew. FY22 outlook raised (mid-teens EPS growth). "Constrained not by orders, but constrained by supply chain."
- 2022-07-26 (Q2-22): FY22 outlook raised again. Targeted capacity expansion in power T&D and communications product lines where capacity is tight.
- 2025-07-29 (Q2-25; file dated 2025-08-04): FY25 adj. EPS raised to $17.65–18.15. Data-center sales expected +30% in 2025; grid-infrastructure orders up high teens in H1; grid automation -13%.
- 2025-10-28 (Q3-25): FY25 EPS raised to $18.10–18.30. Order acceleration in September–October (T&D, data center); Q4 organic growth seen at 8–10%. Revenue guidance trimmed only in the Utility segment (timing).
- 2026-02-03 (Q4-25): Q4 data-center growth above 60%; data center now more than 10% of Electrical segment sales. Order-book inflection since September in T&D and data center. 2026 outlook: 7–9% total sales growth, EPS $19.15–19.85.
- 2026-04-30 (Q1-26): data-center sales ~+40% (balance-of-system components plus modular power skids). FY26 outlook raised to 8–11% total and 6–9% organic growth (~3 points from price); FY data-center growth guide raised to more than 25% from 15% (as restated by an analyst). Capacity additions continue. Early pricing pushback ("enough is enough"); no supply constraints.

Current state (latest call): data-center and T&D demand are strong; the 2026 outlook was raised and the data-center growth guide is above 25%. Price contributes about 3 points of growth, with early customer pushback.
