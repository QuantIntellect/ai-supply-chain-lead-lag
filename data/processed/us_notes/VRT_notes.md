# VRT — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only Q3-2024 and Q2-2025 → Q2-2026 calls are in the data (no 2022–2023, Q4-2024 or Q1-2025 transcripts).

- 2024-10-23 (Q3-24; file mis-dated 2026-04-22): trailing-12-month (TTM) orders +37%, Q3 orders +17% against tough comps; Vertiv moved to reporting TTM orders. FY24 guidance raised again across all metrics (14% organic growth, adj. op. margin ~19%). The pipeline entering Q4 was higher than the one entering Q3.
- 2025-07-30 (Q2-25): first quarter with more than $3bn of orders (+15% y/y, +11% q/q), book-to-bill 1.2x. Backlog $8.5bn (+21% y/y, +7% q/q). FY25 net-sales guidance raised by $550m to $10.0bn.
- 2025-10-22 (Q3-25): Q3 orders +60% y/y and +20% q/q (TTM +21%), book-to-bill 1.4x. Backlog $9.5bn (+30% y/y). Americas organic sales +43% on accelerating AI demand. FY25 sales raised to $10.2bn (27% organic growth). Investment in supply chain and capacity being accelerated.
- 2026-02-11 (Q4-25): order blow-out. Q4 organic orders +152% y/y and +117% q/q (headline "more than 250%"), TTM orders +81%, book-to-bill 2.9x. Backlog $15bn, more than double y/y and +57% q/q, with deliveries in a 12–18-month window. 2026 guidance $13.5bn at midpoint (28% organic growth; Americas high-30s %, APAC mid-20s %, EMEA flat to down mid-single digits). Capex raised to 3–4% of sales (from the historical 2–3%). 2025 pricing beat inflation and the same is expected in 2026. Vertiv will stop disclosing quarterly orders and backlog.
- 2026-04-22 (Q1-26): Americas organic sales +44%, EMEA -29% (after earlier soft orders). FY26 guidance raised to $13.75bn net sales (+34%), $3.2bn adj. op. profit and $6.35 EPS. Longer "pipeline duration" and backlog elongation give visibility into 2027. Orders expected up y/y in 2026; capacity investment accelerated, mostly landing in H2.
- 2026-07-29 (Q2-26; file dated 2026-08-07): sales $3.274bn (+24%), with some Q2 revenue pushed out by "temporary supply chain congestion". FY26 net sales raised $250m to $14.0bn (+37%); EPS $6.70, FCF $2.5bn. Capex at the high end of the range (4% of sales) to build 2027 capacity. Pipeline accelerating across hyperscale, colocation and enterprise. Won VisionBay AI (Taiwan's first NVIDIA GB300 AI data center); collaborating on the first 800V DC AI data center for NVIDIA Vera Rubin.

Current state (latest call): demand is still accelerating. The FY26 sales guide was raised a third time to $14.0bn (+37%). The binding constraints are execution, supply-chain congestion and factory capacity (capex at 4% of sales), not orders. Quarterly order and backlog figures are no longer disclosed.
