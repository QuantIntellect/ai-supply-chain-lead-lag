# SMR — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: three calls (Q3-2025, Q1-2026, Q2-2026). Q4-2025 is missing.

- 2025-11-06: Q3-25. The ENTRA1 Energy–TVA agreement (September 2025) covers up to 6 GW (~72 NuScale modules, up to six plants), with first power as early as 2030, aimed at AI data centers, manufacturing and defense. Signed a partnership milestone agreement with ENTRA1. Doosan can build 20 modules/yr; Fluor is doing FEED for RoPower.
- 2026-05-07: Q1-26 (file dated 2026-05-08). ENTRA1–TVA talks are "advancing well toward a definitive PPA". RoPower (Romania) was advanced by Nuclearelectrica shareholders. Korea is discussing a $350bn package, of which $200bn could go to AI data centers and nuclear. Key suppliers: Framatome (fuel) and Doosan (modules).
- 2026-08-05: Q2-26. Still no definitive TVA PPA: ENTRA1 "continues to advance discussions". Management says "every major hyperscaler" is engaged in the conversation. More than 60 specialized suppliers; Paragon won the safety I&C design contract.

Current state (latest call, 2026-08-05): Demand is indirect, via ENTRA1/TVA (up to 6 GW) and RoPower. No hyperscaler offtake is named, and the definitive TVA PPA is still pending after three calls.
