# APLD — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: the three files dated 2026-01-22 are really two calls, 2024-10-09 (Q1-FY25) and 2025-01-14 (Q2-FY25). The "q4-2024" file carries a 2024-10-09 header but its content is the earlier FQ4-FY24 call (revenue $43.7m); that call's true date is not in the data, so it is used only as context. Calls from Apr-2025 to Jan-2026 are missing.

- 2024-10-09 (Q1-FY25): revenue $60.7m; 286 MW of crypto hosting, fully contracted. The initial 100 MW lease (expandable to 400 MW) with a US hyperscaler at Ellendale is "98.5% or 99% there" after exclusivity lapsed; the earlier FQ4-FY24 call had disclosed an LOI for that 400 MW lease. Three more hyperscalers are pushing for 2025–26 capacity, which is "in extremely short supply". Pipeline above 1.4 GW. NVIDIA joined a post-quarter equity raise.
- 2025-01-14 (Q2-FY25): Macquarie facility of up to $900m for Ellendale, plus a right of first refusal to invest up to $4.1bn more, giving enough equity for more than 2 GW at current costs. Another 1.6 GW of campuses has 2026–27 power. Cited a ~36 GW US data-center power shortfall by 2028.
- 2026-04-08 (Q3-FY26; file dated 2026-04-09): ~$16bn of contracted lease revenue: $11bn from CoreWeave (Polaris Forge 1, 400 MW) and $5.5bn from an investment-grade hyperscaler (Polaris Forge 2, 200 MW). CoreWeave leases restructured with a parent guarantee, and the SPV was rated A3. Broke ground on Delta Forge One (300 MW, operations mid-2027). Four sites (~1 GW) being marketed and ~900 MW under construction. Hyperscalers are "as aggressive as we have ever seen them" and GPU rental rates are rising. The cloud unit is being spun into ChronoScale.
- 2026-10-07 (Q1-FY27; file dated 2026-10-08): ~$36bn of contracted revenue across 5 campuses on 15-year take-or-pay leases. Polaris Forge 3 and Delta Forge 1 & 2 are leased to the same Tier-1 investment-grade hyperscaler; Polaris Forge 1 (400 MW) and 2 (200 MW) are fully funded. PPA with Base Electron (~1.2 GW of gas generation, deliveries from 2030; APLD holds ~10%). Up to 1 GW of power secured in Finland as customers ask about Europe. Goal of 3.5–4 GW operating by 2030; 1.3 GW of US capacity being marketed. ChronoScale has a 50 MW Microsoft GB300 deployment.

Current state (latest call): contracted revenue more than doubled in six months, from ~$16bn to ~$36bn. The constraints are construction capacity and power; demand for European capacity is emerging.
