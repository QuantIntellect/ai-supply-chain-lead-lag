# BE — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: four calls (Q2-2025 to Q1-2026). The Q2-25 call (2025-07-31) is filed under 2026-04-28.

- 2025-07-31: Q2-25. Oracle partnership to power its AI data centers. AEP (which signed a 1 GW fuel-cell agreement in 2024 to power AWS) said AWS and Coralogix are deploying Bloom in Ohio. Quanta Computer (AI servers) ordered an islanded microgrid. Doubling factory capacity from 1 to 2 GW/yr by end-2026; reiterated 2025 guidance. Hyperscaler capex is ">$1 billion a day", and the share of data centers expected to run islanded has risen from 20% to ~40%.
- 2025-10-28: Q3-25. Brookfield partnership: Bloom is the preferred on-site power provider and Brookfield will finance Bloom-sourced AI projects. Equinix has >100 MW deployed. First Oracle AI-factory deal (August). Expects 2025 to beat prior guidance; 2 GW capacity "all systems go".
- 2026-02-05: Q4-25. Product backlog ~$6bn (+140% y/y); service backlog ~$14bn. The backlog includes "half a dozen hyperscale and neo cloud end customers" vs one a year earlier. C&I backlog +135%; >80% of US backlog is now outside California and the Northeast. AEP exercised its fuel-cell option. 2026 guide: revenue $3.1–3.3bn, capex $150–200m.
- 2026-04-28: Q1-26. Oracle Project Jupiter (New Mexico): up to 2.45 GW with Bloom as sole power provider, replacing planned gas turbines and diesel. Raised FY26 revenue guidance to $3.4–3.8bn (from $3.1–3.3bn). Manufacturing footprint now 5 GW/yr. More than half of data-center backlog is from other hyperscalers, neoclouds and colos; turbine rivals' new supply arrives only in 2029+.

Current state (latest call, 2026-04-28): Demand is inflecting. A 2.45 GW Oracle award, backlog up 140%, and the guide raised above the prior range's top. Capacity is scaling 1→2→5 GW/yr, positioned as faster-to-power than turbines.
