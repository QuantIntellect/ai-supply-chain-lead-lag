# CLS - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2026-01-29 to 2026-04-28
(2 calls in sample: Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 only; no 2022-2025 calls available)

- 2026-01-29: Q4 2025 revenue $3.65bn (+44%); CCS $2.86bn (+64%, 78% of revenue); communications end market +79% on 800G switch ramps at the largest hyperscalers; HPS $1.4bn (+72%). FY2025 revenue $12.4bn (+28%).
- 2026-01-29: Raised the 2026 outlook to $17bn revenue (+37%) and adj EPS $8.75; CCS to grow ~50%; Q1 guide $3.85-4.15bn. The capacity build is "a response to record bookings" and "Demand continues to well outstrip our ability to provide it in the very short term."
- 2026-01-29: Capex steps up to ~$1bn in 2026 (6% of revenue) from $201M in 2025: >700k sq ft across Richardson and Fort Worth, Texas, plus Thailand, Mexico and Japan, while still guiding >=$500M FCF.
- 2026-01-29: Concentration: three customers at 36%, 15% and 12% of Q4 revenue. Preferred (primary, not sole) manufacturing partner for Google TPU systems; new 1.6T switch award with a third hyperscaler (production ramp 2027); first 1.6T mass production late 2026.
- 2026-04-28: Q1 2026 revenue $4.05bn (+53%), CCS $3.24bn (+76%); enterprise +101%, but the AI/ML compute ramp was partly gated by component constraints; Q2 guide $4.15-4.45bn.
- 2026-04-28: Raised the 2026 revenue outlook by $2bn to $19bn (+53%), adj EPS $10.15; "our outlook for 2027 has strengthened compared to just 90 days ago"; 10 active 1.6T programs ramp heavily in 2027; the digital-native rack-scale program enters production in 2027.
- 2026-04-28: Supply tightened: "more component shortages now than 90 days ago", with custom silicon and memory on allocation and lead times extending. Many orders are NCNR and backed by long-term capacity agreements, giving visibility into 2028.
- 2026-04-28: New wins: the AMD Helios scale-up switch (design and manufacturing) and a co-packaged-optics 1.6T Ethernet switch on Broadcom Tomahawk for a hyperscaler (mass production 2027). 2027 capex placeholder ~$1.5bn vs ~$1bn in 2026 (Q1 capex $230M).
Current state (latest call): 2026-04-28. Demand exceeds supply, with the 2026 outlook raised to $19bn (+53%) and 2027 visibility strengthening (10 1.6T programs, TPU, digital-native rack, AMD Helios). The limit is components (custom silicon, memory), not demand. Capex is rising toward ~$1.5bn in 2027.
