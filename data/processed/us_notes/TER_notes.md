# TER - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2025-04-29 to 2026-04-29
(4 calls in sample; no 2022-2024 calls and no Oct-2025 call, so the 2022-24 test downturn and the first HBM test ramp are not covered directly. Dates are true call dates.)

- 2025-04-29: Trade-policy uncertainty pushed customer orders out of Q2 (mainly auto/industrial). Q2 sales guide $610-680m vs Q1 $686m, and prior guidance beyond Q2 withdrawn. Memory revenue fell to $109m as customers digest the HBM test capacity bought in 2024, with a further significant decline guided for Q2.
- 2025-04-29: AI bright spots. Record loading on UltraFLEX/UltraFLEX+ testers for AI accelerators; first Titan HP system-level-test revenue from a hyperscaler; HBM4 performance-test win at a major DRAM maker (shipping 2H25). 19% of revenue shipped to China; one customer above 10% of revenue.
- 2025-07-30: Turn. "Demand trends in AI compute have strengthened and forecasts are materializing into orders." Q3 sales guide $710-770m vs Q2 $652m; AI compute expected to be the majority of 2H25 semi-test revenue. Memory ($61m in Q2) expected to snap back in 2H as HBM digestion ends; second HBM4 (singulated-stack) win; Quantifi Photonics acquisition closed (silicon-photonics test).
- 2025-07-30: VIP (hyperscaler custom-silicon) compute is driven by "less than 6, more than 1" customers. Merchant-GPU test was still an opportunity, not yet won (compute TAM ~$2.3bn in 2024).
- 2026-02-03: Inflection. Q4 revenue $1.083bn (+41% QoQ). AI drove more than 60% of Q4 revenue (40-50% in Q3), with upwards of 70% expected in Q1. Memory revenue was a record $206m (+61% QoQ); compute is now ~50% of SoC revenue; 2025 revenue $3.2bn (+13%).
- 2026-02-03: Q1 guide $1.15-1.25bn (a record), backed by "really strong backlog"; 2026 expected ~60% weighted to 1H. SoC test TAM in 2025 was nearly 60% larger than 2024, and the memory test TAM is guided up low double digits in 2026. New HDD test customer ramping in 2026; ~50% VIP compute share. Two >10% specifying customers plus one >10% purchasing customer; MultiLane JV announced; new $6bn revenue target model.
- 2026-04-29: Record Q1 revenue $1.282bn (+87% YoY), with AI at ~70% of revenue. First multi-system merchant-GPU production test orders (~$50m line of sight for 2026). Memory test demand is stronger than the January view on HBM/DRAM; UltraFLEXplus shipments more than doubled in 9 months at 12-16 week lead times.
- 2026-04-29: Caution on 2H. Q2 guide $1.15-1.25bn (below Q1), 55-60% of 2026 revenue in 1H, and limited 2H visibility. Customers are "holding back on the orders for the test equipment" until front-end wafer capacity comes online; 2 hyperscaler compute programs at scale; $6bn target model reiterated.

Current state (latest call): 2026-04-29 - AI is ~70% of revenue and Q1 was a record $1.282bn, but the Q2 guide of $1.15-1.25bn and 55-60% first-half weighting show test orders lagging customers' front-end capacity adds. Merchant-GPU entry (~$50m in 2026) is the new upside lever.
