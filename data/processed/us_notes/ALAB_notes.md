# ALAB - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2026-02-10 to 2026-08-04
(3 calls in sample, all 2026; no calls in the sample before 2026-02, so the 2024-25 ramp is visible only through year-over-year comparisons)

- 2026-02-10: Q4-25 revenue $270.6M (+17% QoQ, +92% YoY); FY25 revenue $852.5M (+115%). Q1-26 guide $286-297M (+6-10% QoQ). Management cited Google and AWS guiding nearly $400bn of 2026 capex combined.
- 2026-02-10: Amazon 8-K warrant: 3.3M warrant shares that vest with payments to buy up to $6.5bn of ALAB smart fabric switches, signal-conditioning and optical products (a follow-on to an earlier warrant). Amazon and NVIDIA (NVLink Fusion hybrid racks) both named ALAB as a partner.
- 2026-02-10: Scorpio P keeps ramping at the unnamed lead customer (custom Grace Blackwell deployments, with Vera Rubin to follow), and shipments start to at least 2 more major hyperscalers. Taurus grew more than 4x in 2025. A Microsoft/Intel/SAP CXL partnership covers Azure M-series. Served market expected to grow more than 10x in 5 years to $25bn; merchant scale-up switching put at about $20bn a year by 2030.
- 2026-02-10: Timing of the next leg is set by customer roadmaps. UALink adoption starts in 2027, with AWS (next Trainium) and AMD (MI500) both committed to UALink in 2027.
- 2026-05-05: Q1-26 revenue $308.4M (+14% QoQ, +93% YoY). PCIe Gen 6 was more than one-third of revenue. Q2 guide $355-365M (+15-18% QoQ). Scorpio X moved from pre-production to its initial volume ramp, and content is now above $1,000 per accelerator.
- 2026-05-05: Supply: "pockets of supply challenges" across the industry, but ALAB has supply in place through year-end and is lining up 2027 supply. New wins: a custom Leo CXL KV-cache design (ships 2027), another new hyperscaler in at-scale tests (revenue 2027), and NVLink Fusion engagements with NVIDIA plus a hyperscaler.
- 2026-08-04: Record Q2-26 revenue $392.4M (+27% QoQ, +104% YoY). PCIe 6 rose to more than 50% of revenue from one-third in Q1. Q3 guide $540-560M, +40% QoQ at the midpoint, as Scorpio X enters high-volume production at the lead customer. Scorpio becomes the largest product family in Q3, one quarter earlier than previously expected.
- 2026-08-04: Memory tightness is now a demand driver: hyperscalers are adopting CXL to cope with memory supply and pricing. New standard Leo design win at a U.S. hyperscaler; Leo volume to 2 U.S. hyperscalers in 2027. A tier-1 AI platform optical program reaches volume in 2027. Taurus market opportunity above $4bn by 2030. China stays in the single digits as a share of revenue.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-04 - growth is accelerating: record revenue of $392.4M (+104% YoY), with a Q3 guide of $540-560M (+40% QoQ) on the Scorpio X volume ramp. Management sees no supply constraint of its own, but memory tightness is pulling CXL demand forward, and the 2027 pipeline (UALink, NVLink Fusion, optics, Leo) is broadening across hyperscalers.
