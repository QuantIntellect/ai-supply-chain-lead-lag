# CRWV — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only three calls (Q4-25 to Q2-26) are in the data.

- 2026-02-26 (Q4-25; file dated 2026-02-27): contracted revenue backlog $66.8bn (+$11.2bn q/q, up more than $50bn y/y). 850 MW of active power delivered in 2025, ~2 GW of infrastructure secured, 1.7 GW targeted for 2026. 2026 capex $30–35bn (more than 2x 2025), substantially all tied to signed take-or-pay contracts. Exit-2026 run-rate revenue $17–19bn, rising to more than $30bn exiting 2027. "Virtually sold out" for 2026. NVIDIA invested $2bn in January.
- 2026-05-07 (Q1-26; file dated 2026-05-08): record bookings of more than $40bn; backlog $99.4bn (+~50% q/q, ~4x y/y), 36% recognizable within 24 months. Ten customers committed to at least $1bn each, including Meta (new $21bn agreement) and newly added Anthropic. Jane Street added $6bn of capacity and invested $1bn; Hudson River Trading and Perplexity are new customers; financial-services backlog is close to $10bn. OpenAI and Cohere contracts financed via delayed-draw term loans. Active power above 1 GW, contracted power above 3.5 GW. Prices rose q/q for A100/H100/H200/L40 and 2026 is "largely sold out". 2026 capex raised to $31–35bn (component pricing); exit-2026 run-rate revenue raised to $18–19bn. Targeting more than 8 GW active by 2030, with access to 5 GW of additional infrastructure.
- 2026-08-11 (Q2-26; file dated 2026-08-18): revenue $2.6bn (+112% y/y). Backlog $104.2bn (+246% y/y), plus more than $25bn of net new commitments early in Q3. Active power 1.5 GW (~+500 MW in the quarter); contracted power 3.7 GW at quarter end and 4.2 GW today. Year-end active power target raised to more than 1.85 GW (from 1.7 GW). 2026 capex raised again to $35–39bn; FY revenue $12.4–13.2bn; exit run-rate revenue $18.5–19.5bn. ~25% price increase across SKUs in July. Long-term supply agreement with Solidigm; IBM named as a customer. An analyst cited a target of more than 3 GW active by end-2027.

Current state (latest call): backlog above $104bn and growing (>$25bn added after Q2). Capacity is sold out and supply-constrained, with prices rising. The 2026 capex guide was raised twice to $35–39bn.
