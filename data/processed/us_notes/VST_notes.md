# VST — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: only three calls in the corpus (Q4-2025 to Q2-2026). Earlier Vistra calls are missing.

- 2026-02-26: Q4-25. ~3.8 GW of nuclear is under long-term contracts. AWS: a 20-year, 1,200 MW deal at Comanche Peak (Amazon brings 1:1 backup generation; initial energization 2027, full ramp by 2032). Meta (January 2026): 20-year PPAs for 2,176 MW of operating capacity at Perry and Davis-Besse plus 433 MW of uprates (deliveries from December 2026 and December 2027; uprates 2031–34). Up to 3.2 GW more is contractible at Beaver Valley and Comanche Peak.
- 2026-02-26: Agreed to buy Cogentrix (~5,500 MW of gas at ~$730/kW), giving a 26 GW CCGT fleet. Sees hyperscaler capex above $700bn in 2026 (~+50% y/y), but says data centers will not meaningfully tighten supply-demand "until late 2027 or early 2028". FY25 adjusted EBITDA ~$5.9bn; >$10bn of cash expected through 2027; eighth straight quarter of a "structurally improved demand environment".
- 2026-05-07: Q1-26 (file dated 2026-06-01). Reaffirmed 2026 guidance, which excludes Cogentrix and the Meta PPA premium. Sees ERCOT growth of ~30–40 GW by 2030; bridge power is now part of customer talks. Priority is contracting ~3.2 GW of nuclear at Beaver Valley and Comanche Peak; Meta-style deals are possible with gas too.
- 2026-08-07: Q2-26. Reaffirmed 2026 adjusted EBITDA $6.8–7.6bn and FCF before growth $3.925–4.725bn. Data-center activity is "strong", with active negotiations, but load mostly arrives in 2028+ (≈2% CAGR from data centers vs 3% from other sectors). ERCOT prices softened while PJM forwards rose; soft 2026 load "not that big of a surprise". Guidance update planned for the Q3 call.

Current state (latest call, 2026-08-07): Contracted hyperscaler book (AWS 1.2 GW, Meta ~2.6 GW) plus active large-load negotiations. Management is explicit that data-center load is a 2028+ story and that ERCOT pricing has softened near term.
