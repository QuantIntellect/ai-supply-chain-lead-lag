# OKLO — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: five calls. The Q3-2024 call (2024-11-14) is filed under 2026-01-22, and its digest section repeats sentences.

- 2024-11-14: Q3-24. Non-binding LOIs with two major data-center providers for up to 750 MW took signed agreements to 2.1 GW (from >700 MW at the AltC merger). Equinix: 500 MW strategic agreement with a $25m power prepayment. Also cited Prometheus Hyperscale and Diamondback Energy deals and a Siemens Energy partnership. Recent nuclear deals are setting baseload prices "at or above $100 per megawatt hour".
- 2025-11-11: Q3-25. Customer pipeline ~14 GW, "predominantly made up of data center and hyperscaler customers". Customers are lengthening horizons because power is constrained. DOE Reactor Pilot Program selections, including Atomic Alchemy. FY25 operating cash use $65–80m.
- 2026-03-17: Q4-25. Meta prepayment agreement supporting up to 1.2 GW at the Aurora-Ohio campus (Pike County); phase 1 is ~150 MW around 2030. Aurora-INL is under construction; Siemens Energy has the power-conversion contract. 2026 guide: operating cash use $80–100m (up from $65–80m in 2025); investing $350–450m.
- 2026-05-11: Q1-26. Named major customer relationships Switch and Meta. NVIDIA–Los Alamos collaboration on fuel validation. Pipeline spans data centers, utilities, industrials, oil and gas, and government.
- 2026-08-07: Q2-26. Raised 2026 PP&E spend to $400–500m (from $350–450m) and operating cash use to $120–150m (from $80–100m). Centrus HALEU LOI covers up to 5 Aurora units, with deliveries from 2029. Kiewit MOU for the Ohio campus EPC. Fuel supply is called the gating constraint.

Current state (latest call, 2026-08-07): Pre-revenue. The anchor data-center customer is Meta (up to 1.2 GW, first 150 MW ~2030), within a ~14 GW mostly hyperscaler pipeline. Spending is being pulled forward; fuel is the bottleneck.
