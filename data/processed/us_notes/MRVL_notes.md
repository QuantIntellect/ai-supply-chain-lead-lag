# MRVL - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-03-03 to 2026-08-27
(16 calls in sample; no calls between 2025-03-05 and 2026-03-05, i.e. the May/Aug/Dec-2025 calls are missing)

- 2022-03-03: Exited FY22 with "record bookings momentum". Q4 data-center revenue $574M (+113% YoY, +15% QoQ), led by cloud. Began volume shipments of 800G PAM4 DSPs for cloud/AI optics; managing an "unprecedented" supply-constrained cycle; new switch win at a Tier-1 cloud customer in Asia (Innovium).
- 2022-08-25: First crack: Q2 DC $643M (+48% YoY), but Q3 DC guided down mid-single digits QoQ, with the cloud re-acceleration pushed to Q4 "primarily on the basis of supply". 800G AI optics demand outran supply; inventory +$78M for new design-win ramps.
- 2022-12-01: Demand turned. "Late in the quarter" customers asked to push out and reschedule orders. Q4 DC guided down mid-20s% QoQ and mid-to-high-teens% YoY (storage-led) after Q3 DC of $627M. Management cited "inventory adjustment going on in digestion" as the "slope of the capex curve has just come down".
- 2023-03-02: Broad-based inventory correction: revenue -8% QoQ; Q1 FY24 guided -8% QoQ / -10% YoY, with more DC storage declines and channel/customer inventory reduction. Digestion headwinds expected to subside through FY24; AWS strategic-supplier collaboration; 800G ramp attributed to AI clusters.
- 2023-05-25: AI inflection: FY23 AI revenue ~$200M, and AI bookings "increased very significantly" since March. FY24 AI to at least double; cloud-optimized (custom) design wins now to "well exceed" the prior $800M/yr projection. Q2 cloud guided >+10% QoQ.
- 2023-08-24: AI exit-rate outlook raised to >$200M/qtr ($800M annualized) for FY24. Q2 DC $460M (+6% QoQ vs a flat guide) on accelerating AI optical demand. Wired carrier still in inventory digestion; capex $111M (leading-node tape-outs).
- 2023-11-30: Q3 DC $556M beat on AI; Q4 DC guided +mid-30% QoQ, with DC to exceed 50% of revenue. AI/cloud offsetting weak enterprise on-prem, carrier and storage (soft Q1 expected); NVIDIA collaboration on optical interconnect.
- 2024-03-07: Q4 DC record $765M (+54% YoY, +38% QoQ). AI >10% of FY24 revenue (vs ~3% the prior year), with Q4 AI well over $200M; FY24 revenue $5.5bn. Custom AI silicon to exceed $200M/qtr exiting FY25. Enterprise/carrier still in inventory correction ("tale of two cities"); TSMC 2nm platform partnership.
- 2024-05-30: Q1 DC record $816M (+87% YoY). AI on track to beat the $1.5bn FY25 "floor" (about two-thirds optics, one-third custom), +$1bn to $2.5bn in FY26. Custom accelerated-compute TAM ~$7bn (CY23) to >$40bn (CY28); DC TAM $21bn to $75bn. Customers "raise their estimates".
- 2024-08-29: Q2 DC record $881M (+92% YoY); Q3 DC guided +high-teens QoQ. "Bookings momentum has been extremely strong", with capacity secured for Q4 and the next fiscal year; FY25 AI to "significantly exceed" target. Enterprise networking orders recovering; Microsoft Azure Key Vault win.
- 2024-12-03: Custom AI ramp: revenue $1.516bn (+19% QoQ); DC record $1.1bn (+98% YoY, +25% QoQ) = 73% of revenue. Q4 guided ~$1.8bn (+26% YoY); set to "significantly exceed" the $1.5bn FY25 AI target. New 5-year multi-generational AWS agreement; supply capacity secured.
- 2025-03-05: Q4 FY25 record revenue $1.817bn (+27% YoY), DC $1.37bn (+78%). FY25 AI "substantially above" $1.5bn; FY26 AI to "very significantly exceed" $2.5bn; Q1 FY26 guided $1.875bn. Lead XPU customer to grow in FY26 and FY27; XPU win at an additional US hyperscaler; Meta custom-NIC win.
- 2026-03-05: FY26 revenue ~$8.2bn (+42%); DC >$6bn (+46%); custom silicon $1.5bn (doubled). Bookings "accelerating at a record pace". FY27 raised to >30% growth, approaching $11bn (vs ~$10bn in Dec-2025), with DC +40% and interconnect >50% (vs 30%). FY28 ~$15bn (~$2bn above Dec). DCI modules to all five major US hyperscalers.
- 2026-05-27: FY27 raised to ~$11.5bn (~+40%), with DC ~+50% and interconnect >70% (vs 50%); FY28 raised to $16.5bn (+$1.5bn). Custom to more than double in FY28, incl. the new Tier-1 XPU program (firm requirements for next year). ~$1bn supplier prepayments to secure capacity: "Everything that touches AI has been constrained". Expanded NVIDIA partnership (optics/silicon photonics, NVLink Fusion).
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-27 - Q2 FY27 revenue $2.739bn (+37% YoY); DC $2.17bn (79% of revenue, +46% YoY). FY27 raised to ~$12bn (from ~$11.5bn) with DC ~+60% (from 50%); FY28 ~$18bn (+$1.5bn) with DC >60%; Q3 guided $3.15bn. Expanded commercial agreement + warrant with a key hyperscaler (TPU-ecosystem programs); ~$1bn capacity prepayments amid "pervasive industry-wide constraints". Demand is still accelerating and supply-limited.
