# SNDK - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2026-01-29 to 2026-08-05
(3 calls in sample, all after Sandisk separated from Western Digital; the earlier flash history is in WDC_notes.md)

- 2026-01-29: NAND turns into a shortage. FQ2 revenue $3,025M (+31% QoQ, +61% YoY) beat the $2,550-2,650M guide on higher prices. "Demand for our products continues to exceed supply": Sandisk could not fulfill customer demand and allocated bits to strategic customers. FQ3 guide $4.4-4.8bn, with the market "more undersupplied" and bits down mid-single digits.
- 2026-01-29: Data-center revenue +64% QoQ ($440M) on PCIe Gen5 TLC drives (qualified at a second hyperscaler); the BiCS8 QLC "Stargate" drive is in qualification with two major hyperscalers. CY26 data-center exabyte growth outlook raised to high-60s% (it was mid-20s, then high-40s, in earlier cycles). Kioxia Yokohama JV extended to 2034. Capex stays disciplined (gross $525M, 8.4% of revenue): any material increase needs multi-year customer financial commitments.
- 2026-04-30: FQ3 revenue $5.95bn (+97% QoQ, +251% YoY) on "extraordinary pricing acceleration". Data-center revenue +233% QoQ ($1.467bn). CY26 data-center growth outlook raised to the mid-70s% from the 60s. Units outside the data center are contracting (rebound expected in '27).
- 2026-04-30: New-business-model (NBM) multiyear contracts: 5 signed; the 3 signed in FQ3 carry ~$42bn of minimum revenue, with >$11bn of financial guarantees and $400M of prepayments. NBMs cover more than a third of FY27 bits. ~$1bn invested in Nanya for DRAM supply. FQ4 guide $7.75-8.25bn.
- 2026-08-05: FQ4 revenue $8,965M (+51% QoQ, +372% YoY). Data-center revenue $2,977M (+103% QoQ); the data center is now 38% of the portfolio vs ~12% of bits a year earlier. QLC Stargate is shipping for revenue.
- 2026-08-05: NBMs now cover 8 data-center and edge customers, with $93.9bn of minimum revenue at floor pricing. RPO is $59.8bn, or $91.1bn including 2 NBMs signed after quarter-end, backed by $16.5bn of guarantees. Sandisk has 4+ years of visibility, and customers are already raising 3-5 year commitments after one quarter.
- 2026-08-05: NAND market seen at >$300bn in CY26 (3x YoY) and ~$500bn in CY27, with the data-center share of TAM going from ~30% (CY25) to ~50% (CY26). Bits stay on allocation beyond CY27. FQ1 FY27 guide $10.3-10.8bn at 83-85% gross margin. Capex 6.3% of revenue.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-05. Sandisk is supply-limited and allocated beyond CY27, with $93.9bn of minimum NBM revenue across 8 customers and the data center at 38% of the portfolio. FQ1 FY27 is guided to $10.3-10.8bn on both bit growth and higher prices, while capex stays ~6% of revenue (supply grows through node transitions, not new fabs).
