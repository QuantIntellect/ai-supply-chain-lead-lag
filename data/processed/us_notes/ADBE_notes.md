# ADBE — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2024-09-12: (This is the first call in the data; no 2022 to mid-2024 calls are available.) Q3-FY24 net new Digital Media ARR $504m; 12bn+ Firefly generations. The Q4 target of ~$550m net new DM ARR was flagged by analysts as the lowest sequential Q4 guide ever, while management called it the "strongest ever Q4 target".
- 2024-12-11: FY24 revenue $21.51bn (+11%) with a record >$2bn of net new DM ARR. FY25 targets: revenue $23.30–23.55bn and DM ending-ARR growth of 11%.
- 2025-03-12: AI-first products (Acrobat AI Assistant, Firefly, GenStudio) passed a $125m book of business exiting Q1-FY25, expected to double by year-end; FY25 targets reaffirmed; DM ARR $17.63bn (+12.6%).
- 2025-06-12: FY25 revenue target raised to $23.5–23.6bn; AI-direct ARR tracking above the $250m target; Firefly services/custom-model ARR up 4x y/y; Firefly integrates third-party models from Google, OpenAI and Black Forest Labs.
- 2025-12-10: FY25 total Adobe ARR $25.2bn (+11.5%). FY26 revenue guide $25.9–26.1bn with ARR growth targeted at 10.2% (~$2.6bn net new). AI-ecosystem integrations with AWS, Azure, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI.
- 2026-03-12: Firefly subscription/credit-pack ARR +75% q/q; generative credit consumption +45% q/q; total ending ARR $26.06bn (+10.9%); revenue growth ticked up to 11% cc.
- 2026-06-11: AI-first ARR >$500m (>3x y/y) and Firefly ARR approaching $300m; full-year revenue and EPS guide raised. The transcript prints the FY26 revenue target as "$20.5–20.6bn", which is inconsistent with the $25.9–26.1bn prior guide and the Q3 target of $6.67–6.72bn, so it is likely a transcription error. Semrush adds ~$480m of ARR.
- 2026-09-10: AI-first ARR >$650m (+150% y/y); FY26 revenue target raised to $26.576–26.626bn; total ARR $27.5bn (+11.2%); credit consumption is accelerating q/q. Amazon is named among customer wins.

Current state (latest call): Total growth is steady at ~11%, but AI-first ARR is compounding (>$650m, +150% y/y) and generative credit consumption keeps accelerating. Firefly runs both Adobe's own models and partner models (Google, OpenAI and others), so inference demand rises with usage.
