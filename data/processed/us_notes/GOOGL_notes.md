# GOOGL — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage: 14 distinct calls, 2022-02-01 to 2026-04-29 (actual call dates). Missing: Q1-23, Q3-23, Q3-24, Q1-25 and Q2-26. The Q3-25 call (2025-10-29) appears as two files and is counted once. Capex is reported (cash-payment) capex, and finance leases are never mentioned.

- 2022-02-01: Management guided 2022 capex to "a meaningful increase", with servers the largest component and office fit-outs re-accelerating. The increase was reiterated on 2022-07-26.
- 2023-02-02: 2023 capex was guided "generally in line with 2022": technical infrastructure up, offices down. This was the pause year.
- 2023-07-25: AI pivot. Technical-infrastructure investment was guided to rise through H2-23 and keep growing in 2024 for GPUs, TPUs and data centers. Q2 spending came in below plan partly because of "delays in certain data center construction projects". More than 70% of gen-AI unicorns were Google Cloud customers.
- 2024-01-30: Q4-23 capex was $11bn, and 2024 was guided "notably larger". Cloud passed $9bn in quarterly revenue with accelerating growth.
- 2024-04-25 to 2024-07-23: Quarterly capex was guided at or above Q1's $12bn (Q2: $13bn). By July, AI infrastructure and gen-AI solutions had "generated billions in revenues" year to date.
- 2025-02-04: 2025 capex was guided to about $75bn, with $16–18bn in Q1 (Q4-24: $14bn). Cloud grew 30% to $12bn and "we do have demand that exceeds our available capacity". Cloud customers were consuming 8x the compute they used 18 months earlier.
- 2025-07-23: 2025 capex was raised to about $85bn from $75bn, and 2026 was guided to "a further increase". Cloud grew 32% to $13.6bn, and backlog reached $106bn (+18% q/q, +38% y/y). Management expects "a tight demand-supply environment going into 2026".
- 2025-10-29: 2025 capex was raised to $91–93bn, and 2026 was guided to "a significant increase" (Q3 capex: $24bn). Backlog reached $155bn (+$49bn q/q, +82% y/y) and Cloud grew 34% to $15.2bn. Anthropic plans to access up to 1 million TPUs. More $1bn-plus deals were signed in the first nine months than in the prior two years. Supply is expected to stay tight through 2026.
- 2026-02-04: 2025 capex came in at $91.4bn, and 2026 was guided to $175–185bn, roughly double. Backlog reached $240bn (+55% q/q, more than 2x y/y), and Cloud grew 48% to $17.7bn. 2025's $1bn-plus deals outnumbered the prior three years combined. Apple chose Google as its preferred cloud provider. Management expects to be "supply constrained" all year. The Intersect data-center and energy acquisition was announced in December.
- 2026-04-29: 2026 capex was raised to $180–190bn to include Intersect, and 2027 capex was guided to "significantly increase" (Q1 capex: $35.7bn). Cloud grew 63% to more than $20bn. Backlog nearly doubled q/q to $462bn, partly because it now includes TPU hardware sold into customers' own data centers; just over 50% converts to revenue within 24 months. The company is "compute constrained in the near term", and Gemini's API is processing 16bn tokens per minute (from 10bn).

Current state (latest call in data, 2026-04-29): Google is compute-constrained. 2026 capex is guided to $180–190bn and 2027 to rise significantly. Cloud backlog is $462bn, including new external TPU hardware sales, and Cloud revenue is accelerating (+63%). The Q2-26 call is not in the dataset.
