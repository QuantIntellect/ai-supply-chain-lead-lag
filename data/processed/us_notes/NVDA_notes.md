# NVDA - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-16 to 2026-08-26
(16 calls in sample; gaps: no 2024-02 call, and no calls between 2025-02-26 and 2025-11-19, so the 2025 H20/China write-down quarters are missing)

- 2022-05-25: Pre-correction peak. Record Data Center (DC) revenue $3.8bn (+83% YoY); hyperscale/cloud revenue more than doubled YoY; networking still supply constrained. Q2 guide $8.1bn included a ~$500M hit from Russia and China COVID lockdowns ($400M Gaming, $100M DC).
- 2022-08-24: Inventory and China down-leg. Q2 revenue $6.7bn missed the $8.1bn outlook; Gaming fell 44% QoQ as channel partners cut inventory; $1.22bn inventory charges. DC $3.81bn (+61% YoY): North America hyperscale grew but China hyperscale fell. Q3 guide $5.9bn.
- 2022-11-16: US export ban on A100/H100 sales to China; $702M inventory charges tied to weaker China DC demand; DC $3.83bn (+31% YoY); Q4 guide $6bn. New cloud deals: multiyear Microsoft Azure AI supercomputer; Oracle OCI adding "tens of thousands" of A100/H100.
- 2023-02-22: Generative-AI inflection. DC $3.62bn (-6% QoQ on China). Activity on Hopper/Ampere for LLM inference "has just gone through the roof in the last 60 days"; CSPs ~40% of DC revenue (trailing 4 quarters); Q1 guide $6.5bn; DGX Cloud launched via OCI, Azure and GCP.
- 2023-05-24: Step-change guide. Q2 revenue guide $11bn vs Q1 revenue $7.19bn (DC $4.28bn), on a "steep increase in demand related to generative AI"; DC visibility extended "out a few quarters"; procured "substantially higher supply for the second half".
- 2023-08-23: Q2 revenue $13.51bn vs $11bn outlook; DC $10.32bn (+171% YoY); Q3 guide $16bn. Supply to increase each quarter through next year; demand visibility extends into next year; China 20-25% of DC; buyers named: AWS, Google Cloud, Meta, Azure, Oracle.
- 2023-11-21: DC $14.5bn (+279% YoY); Q4 guide $20bn. New export controls: sales to China and other affected regions (20-25% of DC) will "decline significantly" in Q4, offset by other regions. GPU-specialist CSPs broaden demand.
- 2024-05-22: DC $22.6bn (+427% YoY); Q2 guide $28bn. "Demand for H200 and Blackwell is well ahead of supply... well into next year." China DC down significantly since the October 2023 controls. Blackwell time-to-market customers: Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle, Tesla, xAI.
- 2024-11-20: Q4 guide $37.5bn. "Blackwell demand is staggering"; on track to beat the prior "several billion dollars" Blackwell estimate (after the August call's mask/yield fix); Oracle clusters of up to 131,000 Blackwell GPUs. Named suppliers: TSMC, SK hynix, Micron, Amphenol, Vertiv, Foxconn.
- 2025-02-26: DC $35.6bn (+93% YoY), FY25 DC $115.2bn; $11bn of Blackwell revenue in its first quarter; large CSPs ~50% of DC revenue; Q1 guide $43bn; reasoning-model inference demand accelerating; China held well below pre-export-control share.
- 2025-11-19: Record DC $51bn (+66% YoY); Q4 guide $65bn. Visibility to $500bn of Blackwell+Rubin revenue through CY2026; "the clouds are sold out". Inventory +32% and supply commitments +63% QoQ; large China orders "never materialized", and the outlook assumes no China DC compute. OpenAI partnership for at least 10GW; Anthropic adopts NVIDIA (up to 1GW).
- 2026-02-25: DC $62bn (+75% YoY, +$11bn QoQ); Q1 guide $78bn. Top-5 clouds and hyperscalers are a little over 50% of DC revenue; Sovereign AI more than tripled to over $30bn in FY26. Supply commitments cover shipments into calendar 2027. $10bn investment in Anthropic; Meta deploying "millions" of Blackwell/Rubin GPUs.
- 2026-05-20: DC $75bn (+92% YoY), hyperscale $38bn (~50%); Q2 guide $91bn. Total supply (inventory, purchase commitments, prepaids) raised to $145bn. Jensen expects to be "supply constrained throughout the entire life of Vera Rubin"; AWS adding more than 1M Blackwell/Rubin GPUs; H200 China licences approved but no revenue yet.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-26. Revenue $96bn and DC $89bn (+18% QoQ; hyperscale $49bn); Q3 guide $108bn. FY28 preliminary revenue growth ~70%, limited by supply (a bottleneck "at least through the end of fiscal year '28"). Memory pricing is "extreme", and OpenAI commitments total ~12GW (4.25GW initial build); no China in the outlook.
