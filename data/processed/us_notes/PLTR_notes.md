# PLTR — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-08-08: Guidance cut. FY22 revenue guide lowered to $1.90–1.902bn ("revisioning guidance down to $1.9 billion") because several of the largest US-government contracts "have been pushed out"; the revised guide excludes any new major USG awards. The May-2022 call had reaffirmed "30% or greater" annual growth through 2025. RPO $1.2bn (+79% y/y); US commercial customers 34→119 y/y.
- 2023-02-13: US commercial revenue +12% y/y to $77m but down q/q as revenue from strategic-investment-program customers fell; FY23 revenue guide $2.18–2.23bn. UK Defence Digital enterprise agreement worth up to £75m.
- 2023-05-08: AIP launch. Karp says demand for AIP is "nothing I've ever seen in 20 years"; US commercial TCV +170% y/y, 155 US commercial customers; adj. operating income guide raised to $506–556m.
- 2023-11-02: NDR fell to 107% on Continental Europe commercial headwinds, while US commercial customers rose to 181 (+37% y/y), helped by AIP go-to-market velocity; RDV $3.7bn, RPO $988m.
- 2024-02-05: "Rapid acceleration and unprecedented demand" in US commercial; US commercial customers 221 (+55% y/y, +22% q/q); FY24 revenue guide $2.652–2.668bn.
- 2024-05-06: FY24 revenue guide raised to $2.677–2.689bn; 915+ organizations through AIP bootcamps. Partnership with Oracle announced; management says hyperscalers realize "in order to drive compute, you need to move beyond chat".
- 2024-08-05: Revenue growth accelerated to 27%; FY24 guide raised to $2.742–2.750bn; DoD CDAO production contract ($153m initial order, up to $480m over 5 years); strongest US-government bookings quarter since 2022.
- 2024-11-04: Revenue +30% y/y (US +44%), beating the top of guidance by ~450bp; FY24 guide midpoint raised to $2.807bn; US commercial customers 321 (+77%).
- 2025-02-03: Revenue +36% y/y; record US commercial TCV quarter (nearly $400m above the prior high); FY25 guide $3.741–3.757bn, US commercial >$1.079bn (+54%). (The Q1-2025 call, May 2025, is not in the data.)
- 2025-08-04: Revenue +48% y/y. "Largest ever" FY25 raise, to a midpoint of $4.146bn (+45%); US commercial >$1.302bn (+85%); TTM US commercial TCV $2.8bn (+141%); Maven Smart System ceiling raised by $795m.
- 2025-11-03: US commercial +121% y/y; FY25 midpoint raised to $4.398bn (+53%) and US commercial to >$1.433bn (+104%); RDV $8.6bn (+91%), RPO $2.6bn (+66%); NDR 134%.
- 2026-02-02: Highest TCV quarter ever ($4.3bn); US revenue +93% y/y; FY26 guide $7.182–7.198bn (+61%), US commercial >$3.144bn (+115%); US Navy shipbuilding contract worth up to $448m.
- 2026-05-04: US business grew at triple digits for the first time; FY26 midpoint raised 10% to $7.656bn (+71%); RDV $11.8bn (+98%), RPO $4.5bn (+134%); NDR 150%; 1,007 customers.
- 2026-08-03: Revenue $1.935bn (+93%); FY26 midpoint raised again to $8.154bn (+82%) and US commercial to >$3.424bn (+134%); Q2 US commercial TCV $2.132bn (+153%, ~$800m above the prior record); RPO $4.9bn (+103%), RDV $13.1bn; NDR 157%. Pivot to "sovereign AI" with an NVIDIA partnership (models fine-tuned on an NVIDIA stack). Gross margin absorbed the cost of taking on cloud hosting for a government customer.

Current state (latest call): Demand is still accelerating. Revenue is up 93% y/y, the FY26 guide has been raised twice (to $8.154bn, +82%), quarterly US commercial TCV hit a record, and RPO is $4.9bn. The next leg is pitched as sovereign-AI stacks built with NVIDIA, which pull PLTR into owning compute and hosting costs.
