# AMAT - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-02-16 to 2026-08-13
(16 calls in sample; no calls between 2025-02-13 and 2026-02-12, so the May/Aug/Nov 2025 calls are missing. Fiscal year ends late October; dates are true call dates.)

- 2022-02-16: Supply-constrained boom. "Demand for semiconductors has never been stronger or broader", but output limited by parts shortages; backlog grew $1.3bn in the quarter with customers booking into 2023; Q2 FY22 guide $6.35bn +/-$300M (~+14% YoY), semi systems ~$4.6bn (+16%). CFO cites TSMC capex of $40-42bn this year and Intel committing to new fabs.
- 2022-08-18: First demand crack. Memory spending in 2023 expected lower on consumer-electronics/PC weakness, but orders stayed strong, backlog rose, customers added four new fab projects, and foundry-logic/ICAPS backlog more than offset memory reductions; demand still "significantly above our ability to supply"; Q4 FY22 guide $6.65bn +/-$400M.
- 2022-11-17: Down-cycle call. CY2023 WFE expected down; new China export rules could cut FY23 revenue by up to $2.5bn; yet record backlog and still supply-limited (metal deposition); Q1 FY23 guide $6.7bn +/-$400M with semi systems ~$5.15bn (+~13% YoY).
- 2023-02-16: Memory capex collapse. "Significant cancellations and pushouts from memory customers" as they rebalance inventories; backlog grew a 9th straight quarter but expected to decline through 2023; ICAPS demand filling the gap; Q2 guide ~$6.4bn +/-$400M.
- 2023-05-18: Trough. Supply caught up (lead times and inventory down), leading-edge push-outs offset by ICAPS; Q3 semi systems guide ~$4.5bn (-~5% YoY); China demand expectations raised; gate-all-around shipments to ramp in 2024; own capex to rise over several years.
- 2023-08-17: AI first framed as a demand driver. HBM is <5% of DRAM capacity but seen growing at ~30% CAGR; DRAM revenue guided up ~$500M QoQ (trailing-edge); ICAPS is the largest slice of 2023 WFE; Q4 guide $6.51bn.
- 2023-11-16: Turn toward AI. 2024 leading-edge foundry-logic demand expected stronger on PC, cloud and AI data-center spending plus the gate-all-around build-out; DRAM pricing and utilization improving; advanced logic to accelerate in 2H CY24; Q1 FY24 guide $6.47bn.
- 2024-02-15: First AI revenue figures. HBM packaging revenue to grow ~4x to almost $0.5bn in FY24; advanced-packaging portfolio ~$1.5bn; HBM (~5% of 2023 DRAM output) now seen at ~50% CAGR; AI GPUs only 6% of leading-edge foundry-logic wafer starts; some ICAPS digestion expected.
- 2024-05-16: AI raise. HBM packaging revenue raised to >$600M (6x) as customers accelerate HBM capacity plans; advanced packaging ~$1.7bn; HBM share of DRAM wafer starts up from ~5% to ~20%; China DRAM shipments (high in 1H) falling off in 2H.
- 2024-08-15: Customer concentration: TSMC a 10% customer in the quarter. Leading logic accelerating on gate-all-around plus AI; HBM packaging >$600M and advanced packaging ~$1.7bn reaffirmed; Q4 FY24 guide $6.93bn (+3% YoY).
- 2025-02-13: China headwinds. New export rules cost ~$400M of FY25 revenue; DRAM faces tough compares as 2024 China purchases do not repeat; foundry-logic +20% YoY (semi systems $5.36bn, +9%); Q2 guide $7.1bn (+7% YoY).
- 2026-02-12: AI capex boom quantified. Semi equipment business to grow >20% in CY26, weighted to 2H and paced by customer cleanroom space; ~2-year customer visibility and "'27 is shaping up to be another strong year"; Q2 FY26 guide $7.65bn (+~9% QoQ); China 27% of semi+AGS sales; first EPIC co-development deal (Samsung).
- 2026-05-14: Raise. CY26 semi equipment growth lifted to >30% as customers find cleanroom space and raised orders in the last 90 days; 8-quarter rolling visibility with large customers, one "worried about the supply all the way into 2030"; Q3 guide $8.95bn (+~23% YoY); TSMC, Micron, Samsung and SK hynix join EPIC as founding partners.
- 2026-08-13: Raised again ("again increased our expectations for 2026 revenue"). Q4 FY26 guide $10.25bn (+51% YoY), semi systems ~$7.9bn (+62% YoY); packaging revenue >70%, process control >50% and AGS >20% growth in CY26; DRAM incl. HBM packaging +52% YoY to a record; hiring to double quarterly system output by 2028; expects another record year in 2027; Broadcom joins EPIC.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-13: demand for AI chips "far exceeds supply". AMAT guided Q4 FY26 to $10.25bn (+51% YoY), raised its CY26 outlook on each of the last two calls, has 8-quarter customer visibility and expects a record 2027; China is 26% of semi+AGS revenue.
