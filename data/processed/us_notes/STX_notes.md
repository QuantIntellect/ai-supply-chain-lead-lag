# STX - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-01-26 to 2026-04-28
(16 calls in sample; no calls between 2023-01-25 and 2023-10-26, so the Apr/Jul-2023 trough quarters are missing. Dates are true call dates.)

- 2022-01-26: Late-cycle strength. Record mass-capacity revenue led by cloud ($2bn, +25% YoY; 111 EB shipped, +56% YoY); FY22 revenue-growth outlook raised to 12-14% from "low double-digit"; March-qtr guide $2.9bn +/-$150m. Non-drive component shortages were delaying customers' system deals; capex at the low end of the 4-6%-of-revenue target.
- 2022-07-21: First crack. Management said the "dynamic market environment is disrupting typical demand patterns"; Sept-qtr guide $2.5bn +/-$150m and FY23 only "flat or even slightly higher". Nearline still 119 EB (+17% YoY) on US-cloud 20TB+ demand; FY22 capex $381m (~3% of revenue); inventory to come down.
- 2022-10-26: Downturn. Nearline shipments 85 EB (-28% QoQ) on cloud and enterprise-OEM inventory adjustments expected to last through calendar year-end; Dec-qtr guide $1.85bn +/-$150m; production output cut and FY23 capex cut below the 4-6% target; China COVID slowdown and weak consumer demand.
- 2023-01-25: Trough. Mass-capacity revenue -10% QoQ to $1.2bn; capex cut by more than 40% QoQ; own inventory down $400m to ~$1.2bn. Early signs that cloud/enterprise customer inventories were trending lower; March-qtr guide $2bn +/-$150m (+6% QoQ); recovery expected in the back half of 2023.
- 2023-10-26: AI crowd-out. CSP spending had "temporarily shifted toward AI-related infrastructure", slowing the storage recovery; cloud inventory correction and China weakness persisted. HDD revenue -6% QoQ to $1.3bn; Dec-qtr guide $1.55bn +/-$150m; HAMR 30TB+ drives in qualification; FY24 capex to be cut significantly vs FY23.
- 2024-01-24: Turn. "Early stages of demand recovery": mass-capacity revenue +4% QoQ to $1.1bn on nearline cloud; price adjustments across the portfolio; March-qtr guide $1.65bn +/-$150m. Expects HAMR (Mozaic) qualifications with a majority of US hyperscalers during CY2024.
- 2024-04-23: Recovery broadens. Expects healthy nearline demand growth through the rest of CY2024; mass-capacity revenue +11% QoQ to $1.2bn; June-qtr guide $1.85bn +/-$150m (+12% QoQ, +16% YoY); continued pricing actions. Sold ASIC assets to Broadcom for $600m.
- 2024-07-23: Tightness returns. Nearline recovery "faster than anticipated", lead times extended, and nearline exabyte supply committed through end of CY2024. Mass-capacity revenue +22% QoQ to $1.4bn; Sept-qtr guide $2.1bn +/-$150m (+44% YoY); price increases under way; FY25 capex at the low end of the target range.
- 2025-01-21: Nearline revenue almost doubled YoY in the Dec qtr (~60% nearline growth for CY2024), matching ~50% higher customer cloud capex in 2024. A production issue puts ~$200m of supply-constrained revenue impact into the March-qtr guide of $2.1bn +/-$150m. AWS cited as endorsing the highest-capacity HDDs.
- 2025-04-29: March-qtr revenue $2.16bn limited by supply. Build-to-order (BTO) visibility with several customers now reaches into 1H CY2026; June-qtr guide $2.4bn +/-$150m (+27% YoY); HAMR ramping at the first hyperscaler, with a second major CSP qualification nearly done.
- 2025-07-29: FY25 revenue +~40% to $9.1bn. Nearline production capacity is "largely spoken for" through mid-CY2026, and demand is "probably stronger than what we have capacity for". Sept-qtr guide $2.5bn +/-$150m (+15% YoY); FY26 capex inside 4-6% of revenue (up from ~3%).
- 2025-10-28: Revenue $2.63bn (+21% YoY) with a record 40.1% gross margin; data-center revenue $2.1bn (80% of total, +34% YoY). High-capacity nearline committed under BTO through CY2026, and LTAs give "clear visibility" through CY2027. Five global CSPs qualified on Mozaic 3+ (>1M Mozaic drives shipped in Sept); Dec-qtr guide $2.7bn +/-$100m (+16% YoY).
- 2026-01-27: Nearline capacity fully allocated through CY2026, with orders for 1H CY2027 to open; LTAs with major cloud customers run through CY2027 and customers are already discussing CY2028. Data-center revenue $2.2bn (+28% YoY); revenue per TB rising; March-qtr guide $2.9bn +/-$100m (+34% YoY). Exabyte growth is to come from areal density, not more units.
- 2026-04-28: Revenue $3.1bn (+44% YoY); data-center revenue $2.5bn (+55% YoY); data-center revenue per TB up mid-single digits YoY. Long-term revenue-growth target raised to a minimum of 20% from low-to-mid teens. Nearline almost fully allocated through CY2027, with BTO contracts fixing configuration and pricing through FY2027 and planning talks reaching CY2028. June-qtr guide $3.45bn +/-$100m (+41% YoY).

Current state (latest call): 2026-04-28 - sold-out, price-rising nearline cycle: capacity almost fully allocated through CY2027, June-qtr guide $3.45bn (+41% YoY), long-term growth target lifted to at least 20% a year; supply grows only through HAMR areal density, not new unit capacity.
