# NVT — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-02-08 (Q4-21): Q4 orders +37% with record backlog; supply-chain cost pressure; carryover pricing of 4–5 points.
- 2022-04-29 (Q1-22): data centers and power utilities +40% in the quarter. 2022 organic guide raised to 11–13% from 6–9%, citing strong orders and record backlog.
- 2022-07-29 / 2022-10-28 (Q2/Q3-22): organic guide raised to 15–17%, then to 18–19%; volume limited by supply-chain constraints. Data Solutions expected to grow only ~3% in 2022. Q3 orders up high single digits; channel partners expected to adjust inventory into Q4.
- 2023-02-07 to 2023-10-27 (destock phase): orders moderated as distributors returned to destocking. Q1-23 orders were down despite good sell-through. Distributors adjusted inventories in Q2-23. Q3-23 organic sales -4% on channel and customer inventory cuts, and the FY23 sales guide was cut to 12–13% from 13–15%. Meanwhile liquid-cooling orders were "accelerating" and capex was raised $15m to $70–75m to add Data Solutions capacity.
- 2024-02-06 (Q4-23): 2023 capex +50% y/y, mostly for capacity ahead of Data Solutions demand. Working with several hyperscale customers on liquid cooling.
- 2024-05-03 (Q1-24): new space in Q3-24 to expand liquid-cooling capacity fourfold. Infrastructure still hit by channel inventory normalization.
- 2024-08-06 (Q2-24): organic orders down y/y on the timing of Data Solutions orders. More capacity investment to support a growing 2025 liquid-cooling backlog; Data Solutions expected above $500m in 2024.
- 2024-11-01 (Q3-24): 2024 Data Solutions sales expected above $575m. Co-designing GB200 NVL72 liquid-cooling solutions with NVIDIA.
- 2025-02-06 (Q4-24): Q4 orders up low teens; backlog $750m exiting 2024; 2025 capex $75–80m. Channel destock was larger than expected.
- 2025-08-01 (Q2-25; file dated 2025-08-06): organic orders up more than 20%; backlog more than 4x y/y. FY25 guide raised to 24–26% reported and 8–10% organic growth.
- 2025-10-31 (Q3-25; file dated 2025-11-02): organic orders ~+65% on large AI data-center orders. FY25 reported growth raised to 27–28%. Expanded capacity due online in 2026.
- 2026-02-06 (Q4-25): orders ~+30%; backlog $2.3bn (3x y/y, about half of 2026 revenue). Data-center sales above $1bn in 2025 (from $600m in 2024). 2026 guide: +15–18% reported, +10–13% organic.
- 2026-05-01 (Q1-26): orders ~+40% (ex-data-center mid-teens); backlog $2.6bn; organic sales +34%. Organic guide raised to 21–23% from 10–13%.
- 2026-07-31 (Q2-26; file dated 2026-08-04): organic sales +47%. Organic guide raised again to 32–34% from 21–23%. 2026 data-center sales seen at ~$2bn (more than double); capex ~$130m (+40%). A third liquid-cooling expansion (Blaine 2, H1-27) is planned because the current expansion "is not going to be enough". However, Q2 orders were only up low double digits and backlog was $2.5bn (vs $2.6bn); management cited lumpy large data-center orders and strong Q3-to-date data-center orders.

Current state (latest call): the 2026 guide has been raised twice (organic 32–34%) and data-center sales are ~$2bn. Watch order lumpiness: Q2 orders grew only low double digits and backlog slipped to $2.5bn.
