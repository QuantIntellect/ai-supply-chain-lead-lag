# ARM - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2023-11-08 to 2026-07-29
(10 calls in sample; listed 2023-09, so nothing before 2023-11; no calls between 2025-02-05 and 2025-11-05)

- 2023-11-08: First call after the IPO. Record revenue (+28% YoY); licensing more than doubled; bookings over $1.1bn, the best quarter in Arm's history; RPO up ~$700M. Demand for its data-center (infrastructure) partner program was "higher than we expected", and generative AI moving to AI clouds is called a "tailwind for Arm".
- 2024-02-07: FY24 revenue guide raised to $3.155-3.205bn (+$160M at the midpoint). Q4 royalties guided up more than 30% YoY, lapping "the bottom of the industrywide inventory correction". Data-center proof points: NVIDIA Grace Hopper, Microsoft Cobalt (128 Neoverse cores) and AWS Graviton.
- 2024-05-08: Q4 revenue $928M (+47% YoY): licensing +60%, record royalty +37%; RPO +45% YoY to nearly $2.5bn. FY25 revenue guided "near $4 billion", with royalty growth in the mid-20% range. Google Axion launched; Grace Blackwell expected to accelerate Arm in AI data centers.
- 2024-07-31: Revenue +39% and licensing +72%, but the FY royalty growth outlook was cut to "low 20-ish percent" from 25%; FY25 revenue guide reiterated. Data center is the strongest area, with Microsoft Cobalt and Google Axion ramping; NVIDIA holds "the lion's share" of accelerators.
- 2024-11-06: Revenue $844M; royalty $514M (+23%); RPO +10% QoQ. FY25 guide of $3.8-4.1bn reiterated. Industrial/IoT inventory correction is ongoing (no IoT recovery until next year). NVIDIA Grace Blackwell shipments started; Cobalt and Axion are generally available; licence dispute with Qualcomm.
- 2025-02-05: Revenue $983M (+19%); record royalty $580M (+23%); FY25 midpoint raised to ~$4bn; Q4 guide $1.175-1.275bn, with licensing ~+60% YoY. Arm is a technology partner in Stargate ($100bn immediately, $500bn total) with OpenAI, Oracle, SoftBank, Microsoft and NVIDIA. AWS: more than 50% of new CPU capacity over two years went to Graviton.
- 2025-11-05: Revenue $1.14bn (+34%); royalty $620M (+21%); data-center royalties doubled YoY; Q3 guide $1.225bn. Compute "demand opportunity is stronger" than at the Stargate launch; constraints are in power and infrastructure. New strategic partnership with Meta; Google is moving most internal workloads to Arm.
- 2026-02-04: Royalties a record $737M (+27%); licensing $505M, including $200M from SoftBank. Data-center royalty +100% YoY and expected to become Arm's largest business within a few years; Q4 guide $1.47bn. Memory shortages may cut low-end smartphone units (seen as a 1-2% royalty impact).
- 2026-05-06: Record revenue $1.49bn (+20%); royalty $671M (+11%), with data-center royalty more than doubling. Arm's own AGI CPU has over $2bn of demand across FY27-28 (2x the launch figure), but the $1bn revenue outlook is held while supply (wafers, memory, packaging, test) is secured: "we're sold out". Target of $15bn AGI CPU revenue by FY31; Meta is lead partner; Q1 guide $1.26bn.
- 2026-07-29: Revenue $1.29bn (+22%), with record Q1 licensing and royalty; data-center royalties more than doubled again. AGI CPU demand now exceeds $2bn, with new US and China customers; manufacturing capacity secured for the initial $1bn. Supply chain "pretty tight" through calendar 2027. Smartphone slowdown from higher memory prices: Q2 guide $1.38bn, with royalty growth guided to 13% (below earlier internal expectations) offset by higher licensing.
Current state (latest call): 2026-07-29. AI/data-center royalties keep doubling YoY and AGI CPU demand exceeds $2bn (supply secured only for $1bn, chain tight through CY2027), while memory-driven smartphone weakness caps near-term growth (Q2 guide $1.38bn, ~22% YoY).
