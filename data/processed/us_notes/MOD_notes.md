# MOD — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only two calls are in the data, both mis-dated 2026-05-25 (actual dates 2025-10-29 and 2026-02-05). Nothing from before FY26 or after Q3-FY26.

- 2025-10-29 (Q2-FY26): data-center sales +42% (+$67m), driving Climate Solutions organic growth of +15%. Climate Solutions outlook raised to +35–40% and data-center sales to more than +60% for FY26 (>90% y/y in H2). Target of more than $2bn data-center revenue by FY28. Margin hit of 225–250bp ($10–12m) from the data-center capacity ramp. New capacity in the US (Franklin, Dallas), Chennai (India) and the UK. Two hyperscalers make up the majority of data-center revenue.
- 2026-02-05 (Q3-FY26): data-center revenue +78% (+$130m); record order intake, roughly 50/50 chillers vs other data-center products. FY26 guide raised again: sales +20–25%, Climate Solutions +40–45%, data center more than +70% (~$1bn data-center revenue this FY). Data-center growth of 50–70% a year projected for the next two years, with an implied ~$1.6bn data-center exit run-rate. Capex $150–180m. Willing to sign long-term agreements (LTAs) for all capacity; capacity for the high end of the range to be in place by end-FY27. Cut production with some new hyperscaler chiller customers during pilot builds.

Current state (latest call): as of the latest call in the data (2026-02-05), orders are at a record, the guide has been raised twice, and 50–70% annual data-center growth is guided for FY27–28. The constraint is the capacity ramp, with LTAs offered to anchor customers.
