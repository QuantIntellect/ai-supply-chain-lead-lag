# PWR — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

Coverage note: only four calls (Q2-25 to Q1-26) are in the data; the digest is thin, so figures come from the transcripts.

- 2025-07-31 (Q2-25; file dated 2025-08-04): record backlog $35.8bn. FY25 guide raised (revenue $27.4–27.9bn), helped by the Dynamic Systems acquisition (~30% of its work historically data centers). Customer demand described as "as strong as ever".
- 2025-10-30 (Q3-25): record backlog $39.2bn, led by accelerating electric-segment demand. FY25 revenue raised to $27.8–28.2bn. NiSource engaged Quanta (50/50 JV with Zachry) to deliver ~3 GW of generation for a large-load data-center customer, with revenue mainly 2027–28. Expects record backlog and double-digit EPS growth in 2026.
- 2026-02-19 (Q4-25): record year-end backlog $44bn. FY25 revenue $28.5bn (+20%). Data centers ~10% of the business, with data-center craft labor the tightest market.
- 2026-04-30 (Q1-26): record backlog $48.5bn, broad-based. FY26 guide raised to revenue $34.7–35.2bn, adj. EBITDA $3.49–3.65bn, EPS $13.55–14.25. March-31 Investor Day: earnings power to more than double by 2030. Hyperscalers turning to bridge power; combined-cycle gas turbine orders placed today would mean 2033 delivery.

Current state (latest call): backlog at a record $48.5bn (up ~$13bn in three quarters) and the FY26 guide was raised. Craft labor, not demand, is the binding constraint for data-center work.
