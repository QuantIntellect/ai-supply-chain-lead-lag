# LRCX - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2022-01-26 to 2026-10-21
(15 calls in sample; the Apr-2022 and Apr-2023 calls are missing, and so are the Apr/Jul/Oct-2025 calls. Lam guides on calendar-year WFE, the industry's wafer-fab-equipment spending; dates are true call dates.)

- 2022-01-26: Supply-limited upcycle. CY22 WFE seen ~$100bn "with strong growth across all segments", >20 new fabs being built; "Customer demand is decently stronger than what we can currently supply"; deferred revenue could grow by up to $500M more as shipments slip; Mar-Q guide $4.25bn +/-$300M held down by parts shortages.
- 2022-07-27: First cut. CY22 WFE lowered to low-to-mid $90bn (from $100bn), framed mainly as industry supply constraints, while memory customers publicly turn cautious on capex; Sep-Q guide still up at $4.9bn +/-$300M.
- 2022-10-19: Downturn plus China. New US export rules cost $2-2.5bn of CY23 revenue; CY23 WFE to fall >20%, mostly memory; deferred revenue and backlog to come down by year-end; Dec-Q guide $5.1bn.
- 2023-01-25: Memory bust. NAND and DRAM inventories "remain very high", and customers are cutting both capacity additions and fab utilization; CY23 WFE mid-$70bn vs mid-$90bn in 2022; Mar-Q guide stepped down to $3.8bn +/-$300M.
- 2023-07-26: AI enters the WFE model. Each +1pt of AI-server penetration is put at $1-1.5bn of extra WFE; CY23 WFE tracking mid-$70bn with upside from domestic-China and HBM demand; memory WFE down mid-20s%, non-memory ~-10%; further NAND cuts heard; Sep-Q guide $3.4bn.
- 2023-10-18: Trough. CY23 WFE raised to ~$80bn (from mid-$70bn) on China; NAND still weak, with low utilization and tool upgrades on hold until inventory digests, but NAND bit-demand growth lifted from high-single-digit to high-teens %; HBM business expected to grow strongly in 2024.
- 2024-01-24: First AI shipment number. HBM-related DRAM and packaging shipments to more than triple in 2024; CY24 WFE early view mid-to-high $80bn; China (mostly domestic customers) stable in 2024; Mar-Q guide $3.7bn.
- 2024-04-24: CY24 WFE raised to low-to-mid $90bn (mainly China lithography); advanced-packaging shipments >$1bn in 2024 and HBM/TSV shipments >3x; "strong setup" for 2025 NAND spending.
- 2024-07-31: CY24 WFE ~mid-$90bn with extra HBM and China demand; the ~$1bn advanced-packaging/HBM figure "has moved higher"; AI training "driving strong demand for GPUs and HBM"; DRAM tool demand to grow through 2025 and beyond; Sep-Q guide $4.05bn.
- 2025-01-29: CY25 WFE ~$100bn; gate-all-around plus advanced-packaging shipments >$3bn in 2025; December-2024 China restrictions remove ~$700M of forecast shipments; Mar-Q guide $4.65bn.
- 2026-01-28: AI capex boom. CY25 WFE came in near $110bn with record Lam revenue >$20bn; initial CY26 WFE ~$135bn, second-half weighted; "basically every customer is asking for pull-ins"; Mar-Q guide $5.7bn +/-$300M.
- 2026-04-22: Raise #1. CY26 WFE $140bn (from $135bn) with upside bias; lead times "stretching out"; customers squeezing tools into scarce clean-room space; AI data centers lifting NAND demand; Jun-Q guide $6.6bn +/-$400M.
- 2026-07-29: Raise #2. CY26 WFE low-$150bn (from $140bn); FY26 record revenue $23.2bn, and NAND revenue more than doubled QoQ on AI storage; Sep-Q guide $8.1bn (>20% QoQ); customers report "unprecedented long-term demand visibility"; inventory built to $4.3bn; the $100bn data-center capex = $8bn WFE rule "probably a little bit of a low estimate".
- 2026-10-21: CY26 WFE mid-$150bn; preliminary CY27 WFE >$200bn (growth led by DRAM, then leading-edge foundry-logic, then NAND); CY26 systems revenue +>40%; Dec-Q guide $8.6bn +/-$400M, held back by supply-chain constraints; clean-room and component limits seen persisting into 2027; customer visibility through 2028.
Current state (latest call): 2026-10-21: supply-constrained AI upcycle. CY26 WFE is ~mid-$150bn and CY27 is seen at >$200bn, with Lam's CY26 systems revenue up >40%. The Dec-Q guide ($8.6bn) is capped by component and clean-room constraints rather than demand, and customer visibility runs through 2028.
