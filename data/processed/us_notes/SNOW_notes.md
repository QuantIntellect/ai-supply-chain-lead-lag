# SNOW — demand notes from earnings calls (2022–2026)

- 2022-03-02: FY23 product-revenue guide $1.88–1.90bn (+65–67%); NRR 178%; record Q4 bookings. Warns of a ~$160m revenue impact from performance improvements, partly offset by $60m+ of new workloads.
- 2022-05-25: Some customers, "most notably consumer facing cloud companies", consumed less than anticipated. FY23 guide nudged to $1.885–1.90bn; NRR 174%.
- 2022-11-30: FY23 guide $1.919–1.924bn, but consumption weakness among SMB customers, and analysts note the Q4 product-revenue guide came in below consensus; NRR 165%.
- 2023-03-01: FY24 product-revenue guide ~$2.7bn (+40%) after "bookings reticence", lower new-logo bookings and slower ramps from young cohorts; NRR 158%.
- 2023-05-24: FY24 guide cut to ~$2.6bn (+34%). Management says economic anxiety is "artificially constraining the demand"; product gross margin helped by more favorable pricing from cloud service providers.
- 2024-02-28: FY25 product-revenue guide ~$3.25bn (+22%, vs +38% in FY24), with headwinds from efficiency gains, tiered storage pricing and Iceberg adoption; ~$50m of GPU-related costs planned for FY25.
- 2024-05-22: FY25 guide raised to ~$3.3bn (+24%); margin guide lowered on increased GPU-related costs for AI; 750+ customers developing on the AI platform.
- 2024-08-21: FY25 raised to ~$3.356bn (+26%). GPU supply bites: "still waiting in some deployments for GPUs" that were expected in the quarter.
- 2024-11-20: FY25 raised to ~$3.43bn (+29%); Anthropic partnership (Claude models in Cortex AI); 1,000+ AI/ML use cases in production.
- 2025-02-26: FY25 product revenue $3.5bn (+30%); FY26 guide ~$4.28bn (+24%); 4,000+ customers using AI/ML weekly; expects "easier GPU access" to help margins. (The Q1-FY26 call, May 2025, is not in the data.)
- 2025-08-27: FY26 guide raised to $4.395bn (+27%); record 50 new $1m customers (654 total); NRR 125%; AI influenced nearly 50% of new logos; GPT-5 available day one in Cortex. (The Q3-FY26 call, Dec 2025, is not in the data.)
- 2026-02-25: FY27 guide $5.66bn (+27%); 9,100+ accounts using AI, and Snowflake Intelligence deployed at 2,500+ accounts (nearly doubling q/q); $200m expanded OpenAI partnership; partnerships with Anthropic, Google Cloud and SAP.
- 2026-05-27: FY27 raised to $5.84bn (+31%). New five-year, $6bn AWS commitment (more than double the prior agreement); Cortex Code at 7,100+ accounts; NRR 126%.
- 2026-09-02: FY27 raised to $6.07bn (+36%); Q3 guide +37–38%; "meaningful step up in AI revenue"; RPO $9bn. FY27 product gross-margin guide cut to 74% because AI workloads carry a lower contribution margin.

Current state (latest call): Growth is re-accelerating, from a +22% FY25 guide to a +36% FY27 guide and a +37–38% Q3 guide, with AI revenue stepping up. The compute intensity of AI workloads now shows in margins (product GM 74%), and SNOW has doubled its AWS spend commitment to $6bn over five years.
