# SMCI - AI demand notes from earnings calls, 2025-02-11 to 2026-08-11
(3 calls in sample: 2025-02-11, 2026-05-05, 2026-08-11; no 2022-2024 calls and none between 2025-02 and 2026-05)

- 2025-02-11: Hopper-to-Blackwell transition air pocket: FY25 (Jun-25) revenue guide cut to $23.5-25bn from $26-30bn; Q3 FY25 guide $5-6bn. Management cited the 10-K delay and Blackwell supply timing.
- 2025-02-11: Still targeted ~$40bn for FY26 ("potential to reach $40 billion"), with growth "assuming that supply chain can keep pace with demand". B200 HGX in volume and GB200 NVL72 racks ready; DLC capacity 1,500 racks/month; xAI Colossus cited as the flagship liquid-cooled build.
- 2026-05-05: Q3 FY26 revenue $10.2bn (+123% YoY, -19% QoQ) on CPU/GPU/memory shortages and customer site readiness (power and networking not ready). Backlog is at "another record high"; AI GPU platforms are >80% of revenue.
- 2026-05-05: Input inflation: memory and SSD prices "double, triple, more than triple" in six months, plus a CPU shortage "especially from Intel". FY26 guide $38.9-40.4bn; Q4 guide $11-12.5bn; one data-center customer is 27% of revenue; capacity >6,000 racks/month.
- 2026-08-11: Pre-announced >$60bn of new Q4 FY26 orders, taking backlog to a record. FY26 revenue $39.1bn (+78%); Q4 $11.1bn came in at the low end on customer delays in power, cooling and networking.
- 2026-08-11: FY27 revenue guide $65-72bn (vs $39.1bn in FY26); Q1 FY27 guide $14.5-15.5bn. ~70% of the $60bn of orders is "pure AI". 9 customers >$1bn in FY26 vs 4 in FY25; the largest customer was 28% of FY26 revenue; inventory of $12.9bn built for FY27.
- 2026-08-11: Platforms: volume GB300 NVL72 and HGX B300, with first-to-market Vera Rubin NVL72 in preparation; AMD Helios/MI450 line launched; Intel Xeon 6+ in volume. Capacity on track for >6,000 racks/month, including >3,000 DLC.
Current state (latest call): 2026-08-11. Backlog is at a record after >$60bn of Q4 orders, and the FY27 guide is $65-72bn. The binding constraints are customer data-center readiness (power, cooling, networking) and component supply and cost. AI share of revenue is swinging (60% in Q4 vs >80% in Q3).
