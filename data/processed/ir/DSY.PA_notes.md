# Dassault Systèmes (DSY.PA): demand and AI narrative, 2023–2026

Basis: company press releases and the company's own call transcripts ("conference call text").
Figures are non-IFRS, and growth rates are in constant currency (cc) unless noted. Times are Paris local.
Since 2019 every scheduled release has gone out at about 07:00 Paris, before the market opens (BMO).

## Dated bullets

- **2023-02-02 (Q4/FY22, 06:59):** Strong close to 2022. Q4 revenue was EUR 1,584m (+10% cc) and cloud software grew 22%. The FY23 revenue objective was set at EUR 5.925–5.975bn. On AI, the company talked about virtual twins feeding "AI engines" and gave no monetization figures.
- **2023-06-09 (CMD, release 06:59):** Set a target of doubling non-IFRS EPS by 2028, to EUR 2.20–2.40. Named Pascal Daloz CEO from 2024-01-01.
- **2023-10-25 (Q3 23):** Revenue grew 11% cc. 3DEXPERIENCE revenue rose 46% on a mega-deal, and Industrial Innovation rose 18%. Life Sciences slowed to +3%, the first sign of Medidata softness.
- **2024-02-01 (Q4 23):** Introduced the "Generative Economy" 2040 horizon. The FY24 objective was EUR 6.35–6.425bn (+8–10% ex FX). Life Sciences grew only 2% in Q4.
- **2024-07-09 (profit warning, 06:59, BMO):** Q2 revenue came in below objectives. The FY24 growth target was cut to 6–8% ex FX (from 8–10%), and EPS growth to 8–11% (from 10–12%). The EUR 6.26–6.335bn range followed with the Q2 results on 07-25. Life Sciences fell 3% in Q2 as the Medidata / clinical-trial (CRO) slowdown continued. A preliminary call was held at 14:00.
- **2024-10-24 (Q3 24):** The FY24 objective was trimmed again, to EUR 6.155–6.275bn (+5–7% ex FX). Industrial Innovation fell 1% against the mega-deal comparison, while Mainstream (SOLIDWORKS) grew 15%.
- **2025-02-04 (Q4 24):** Q4 revenue grew 7% cc and 3DEXPERIENCE grew 22%. The company unveiled **3D UNIV+RSES** (virtual twins combined with AI across the product lifecycle) and AI-based services. The FY25 objective was EUR 6.55–6.65bn (+6–8% ex FX).
- **2025-04-24 / 2025-07-24 (Q1/Q2 25):** "Sovereign infrastructure", including national high-performance and AI data centers, and "AI-driven cloud infrastructure" were named as fast-growing end-markets. This is DS selling into the AI-factory build-out, with no revenue quantified. Subscription revenue grew 10% in Q2. The FY25 guidance was kept at +6–8% ex FX.
- **2025-06-06 (CMD 2025, release 07:00):** Set a target of doubling EPS by 2029. 3D UNIV+RSES was presented as the next growth engine.
- **2025-10-23 (Q3 25):** The FY25 growth outlook was cut to 4–6% ex FX (from 6–8%) on weak Life Sciences (−3%) and CENTRIC PLM, even though subscription grew 16% and 3DEXPERIENCE grew 16%. The company launched three AI-native categories: Virtual Twin as a Service, Generative Experiences, and Virtual Companions (e.g. "Aura").
- **2026-02-11 (Q4 25):** Q4 revenue grew only 1% cc, with 3DEXPERIENCE down 3%. FY26 guidance was set at +3–5% ex FX (EUR 6.29–6.41bn). The company announced an **NVIDIA partnership** (virtual twins plus accelerated computing, "Industry World Models"). It is moving from seat-based to value-based pricing for AI solutions, and called 2026 the year to monetize 3D UNIV+RSES.
- **2026-04-23 (Q1 26):** Revenue grew 3% cc (−4% reported). The company called it a "clear inflection point as AI redefines operational excellence", with customers moving from AI pilots to industrial-scale, agentic deployments. It still disclosed no AI revenue.
- **2026-07-23 (Q2 26):** Revenue grew 4% cc. Cloud software grew 14% and reached 28% of software revenue, and 3DEXPERIENCE grew 14%. The company described 3DEXPERIENCE as an "agentic platform". It announced the acquisition of **ArisGlobal**, an AI platform for life sciences, the same day. FY26 guidance of +3–5% ex FX was confirmed.
- **AI intensity in DS's own call texts:** "AI" appeared about 2–6 times per call in 2022–2024. That rose to 11–13 (H1 2025), then 24 (Q3 25), 21 (Q4 25), 16 (Q1 26) and 54 (Q2 26). Over the same period, organic growth slowed from about 8–11% cc (2022–23) to 1–5% (2025–26). The AI narrative is ahead of measurable monetization.

## Sources and gaps
- **Event timestamps (2018Q4 onward):** taken from investor.3ds.com news-release pages, e.g. "October 23, 2025 at 1:00 AM EDT", which is 07:00 Paris. The IR calendar confirms "press release distributed at 7am CET".
- **Event timestamps (2015–2018Q3):** 3ds.com newsroom `article:published_time` reads a uniform 07:00 Paris, which may be a default value. In the events CSV these times were blanked during cross-company harmonisation. Timing stays BMO, based on DS's documented 7am CET distribution practice and the same-day morning webcast.
  - BMO is still well supported. Each press-release dateline falls on the same day as the webcast, which ran at 09:30/10:00 Paris.
  - The investor.3ds.com timestamps for these years are import artifacts (e.g. 3:24 PM, or the day before), so they were not used.
- **CMD timing:** the 2016 and 2018 CMD release times are unknown (timing = UNKNOWN). The 2019 Life Sciences Day (New York, investor day) is coded as a CMD.
- **KPIs:** cover 2022Q1–2026Q2 and are parsed from the press-release text.
  - DS gives no orders, backlog or gross margin, so those columns are blank.
  - Some segment growth fields are missing for 2022 (reporting was by brand then) and for a few quarters where the wording differs. They are blank rather than estimated.
- **Documents:** press releases and call texts for 2014Q4–2026Q2, presentations for 2022Q1–2026Q2, the CMD 2022/2025 decks, and the July 2024 preliminary release and call script.
  - The 2023 CMD deck was not found on the IR site.
