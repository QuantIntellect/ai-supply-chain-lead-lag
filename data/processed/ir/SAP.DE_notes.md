# SAP SE (SAP.DE / NYSE: SAP) - demand narrative, applications layer

Figures are from SAP's quarterly statements and results presentations (data/processed/ir_text/SAP.DE/), as reported. CCB = current cloud backlog, cc = constant currencies.

## Dated bullets, 2023-2026

- **2023-05-16, 17:47 CEST (after the Xetra close):** At Sapphire, SAP raised its mid-term Ambition 2025 and announced a buyback of up to EUR 5bn. AI was not yet a revenue line.
- **2023-07-20, 18:05 CEST:** This was SAP's first evening release. In Q2-23 the CEO first framed AI as the opportunity: "significant opportunities ahead, in particular through the transformative power of AI". SAP Business AI was pitched as a premium offering. CCB was EUR 11.5bn, +25% cc.
- **2023-10-18, 22:05 CEST:** In Q3-23 the CEO credited the new Business AI capabilities (the Joule copilot, Sept 2023). Cloud gross margin expanded. CCB was +25% cc.
- **2024-01-23, 23:14 CET (ad hoc) and 23:53 CET (Q4 release):** SAP announced a 2024 transformation program covering about 8,000 positions. It shifts investment to "strategic growth areas, above all Business AI", and Ambition 2025 was updated. CCB was EUR 13.7bn, +27% cc, on "stellar order entry".
- **2024-07-22:** In Q2-24, "Business AI enabling many deals", and the 2025 operating-profit ambition was raised.
- **2024-10-21:** In Q3-24, "a significant part of our cloud deals in Q3 included AI use cases". SAP Knowledge Graph was announced and the FY24 outlook raised. CCB was EUR 15.4bn, +29% cc.
- **2025-01-28, 06:02 CET (before the open; Q4 releases moved back to the morning):** Half of Q4-24 cloud order entry included AI. Total cloud backlog was EUR 63.3bn (+40%). The 2025 outlook was cloud revenue EUR 21.6-21.9bn (+26-28% cc).
- **2025-02-13:** SAP launched Business Data Cloud (with Databricks) as the data foundation for AI. In the Q1-25 release (2025-04-22) SAP kept its outlook despite "elevated levels of uncertainty" (tariffs). CCB was +29% cc.
- **2025-07-22:** In Q2-25 Joule became available "everywhere and for everything", with Business Data Cloud called "a powerful accelerator of AI". CCB growth eased to +28% cc.
- **2025-10-22:** In Q3-25 cloud revenue was +27% cc, but CCB slowed to +27% cc. FY25 cloud revenue was now guided to the lower end of its range and operating profit to the upper end. This is the first sign of slower cloud-bookings conversion, even with "AI at accelerated pace".
- **2026-01-29, 06:05 CET:** Business AI was in two thirds of Q4-25 cloud order entry. 90% of the 50 largest Q4 deals included Data or AI. Total cloud backlog reached EUR 77.3bn (+30%), but CCB growth was +25% cc. The 2026 outlook was cloud revenue EUR 25.8-26.2bn (+23-25% cc), with revenue growth to accelerate through 2027.
- **2026-04-23:** The Q1-26 outlook was maintained but now assumes a near-term de-escalation in the Middle East. Total-revenue acceleration was pushed to 2027. SAP announced the acquisition of Reltio (master data management) on 2026-03-27 to make enterprise data "AI-ready".
- **2026-05-12/13:** SAP launched its "Autonomous Enterprise" strategy (Autonomous Suite, Business AI Platform, Joule agents). Financial Analyst Conference at Sapphire Orlando: "AI agents don't work without a brain. The brain is SAP."
- **2026-10-21, 22:07 CEST:** In Q3-26 SAP had its first quarter above EUR 10bn of revenue (+13%, +12% cc). Cloud was +25% cc and CCB EUR 24.1bn (+26% cc). FY26 cloud and cloud-plus-software revenue are now guided to the upper end. Joule Work has more than 110k desktop users.

**Read-through for the AI supply chain:**
- SAP monetizes AI mainly by bundling it into cloud ERP (RISE/GROW) deals and premium AI units, not as a separate revenue line.
- The demand signals are the AI share of cloud order entry (about 50% in Q4-24, two thirds in Q4-25) and CCB growth, which has held at about 25-29% cc since 2023.

## Timing and sources (for event studies)

**Release-time regimes:**
- Through Q1-2023, regular releases came out at about 07:00 CET/CEST, before the Xetra open, followed by a 10:00 press conference and a 14:00 analyst call.
- Q2-2023 was released at 18:00 CEST.
- Since Q3-2023, Q1-Q3 come out at about 22:05 CEST, after both the Xetra and NYSE closes. Q4/FY releases are at about 06:00 CET, before the open.
- An exception: Q4-2023 came out early, on 2024-01-23 at about 23:00 CET, together with the transformation-program ad hoc.

**Sources for each time:**
- **2022-07 onward:** SAP News Center publication time (WordPress API `date_gmt`), cross-checked against SAP's "SAP to release ..." schedule posts and the IR calendar.
- **2016-2022:** news.sap.com `article:published_time` from Wayback snapshots, kept only when the post date equals the release date in the EDGAR 6-K dateline.
- **Ad hoc pre-announcements and warnings:** EQS/DGAP timestamps, mirrored on sap.com IR ad hoc pages.

**Rows without a stamped time:**
- 2015 releases and Q1-2020 have no time. They are BMO by SAP's documented 07:00 practice; the 2015 News Center times are inconsistent with the DGAP stamps, so they were not used.
- CMD/Financial Analyst Conference rows have the date only (timing UNKNOWN). The 2024-2026 FACs were held during Sapphire Orlando, in US hours.

**Pre-announcements:** SAP pre-released its numbers many times: 2015-01, 2015-10, 2016-01, 2016-04, 2019-10, 2020-04 (outlook cut), 2020-07, 2020-10 (Sunday-evening outlook cut), 2021-01, 2021-04, 2021-10 and 2022-01. For these the effective news date is the prelim, not the later full release.

**Weekend events:** For Sunday events (2020-10-25), the first trading day is the next Monday.

**NYSE ADR (SAP):**
- Timing classes are relative to Xetra (09:00-17:30 CET).
- Three rows fall inside NYSE trading hours: 2023-05-16 17:47 CEST (11:47 EDT), 2023-07-20 18:05 CEST (12:05 EDT) and 2015-01-12 16:06 CET (10:06 EST; intraday on Xetra too). For the ADR, day 0 is that same day.
- All other stamped rows are outside NYSE hours: the 22:05 CEST slots, the 19:19-00:15 pre-announcements and warnings, and the 06:00/07:00 CET morning releases.

**CMD rows:** Possible earlier SAP Capital Markets Days (2015-2018) were not verified. Only the 2019-11-12 New York CMD and the 2024-2026 Financial Analyst Conferences are listed.

## Gaps

- No company call transcripts: SAP publishes only webcasts and replays.
- Orders are not disclosed, so `orders` is blank; `backlog` = CCB.
- `gross_margin_pct` is computed as IFRS gross profit divided by total revenue (flagged in extra_json).
- `guidance_rev_low/high` is the cloud and software revenue outlook. SAP gives no total revenue range.
- No transcripts or KPIs before 2022. Pre-2022 quarterly statements are on EDGAR as 6-K exhibits (URLs are in the events file).
