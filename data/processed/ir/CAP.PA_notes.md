# Capgemini (CAP.PA) — IR notes

Capgemini reports revenue only for Q1 and Q3, full results for H1 and FY (with Q2/Q4 growth and bookings). Since mid-2022 it has published at 07:00 Paris time, before Euronext opens. It used to alternate between 07:00 and evening (17:35–17:45) releases; see the timing notes below. Figures are in EUR, "cc" means constant currency, and "GenAI share" is the share of Group bookings.

## Demand / AI narrative (2023–2026)

- **2023-02-21 (FY22):** A record year: revenue of €22.0bn, +16.6% cc and +15.3% organic; bookings +16.8% cc, book-to-bill 1.08. FY23 guidance was cc +4% to +7%, with a 13.0–13.2% margin.
- **2023-07-28 (H1 23):** Growth slowed (Q2 +5.2% cc, +4.7% organic) and Q2 bookings were up only +1% cc. Capgemini announced **€2bn to be invested over 3 years in AI/GenAI**.
- **2023-11-07 (Q3 23):** Q3 revenue was +2.3% cc and -1.3% reported. Demand for **generative AI accelerated, with >100 GenAI projects signed**, and a Gen AI campus was launched.
- **2024-02-14 (FY23):** Q4 revenue was -0.2% cc. Financial Services and TMT were in visible decline. FY24 guidance was set at cc 0% to +3%. GenAI was described as "top of mind for all large organizations". Partners named were Microsoft, Google, AWS, Salesforce and Mistral AI.
- **2024-07-26 (H1 24):** Q2 revenue was -1.9% cc. The **FY24 growth target was cut to -0.5% to -1.5%** (was 0% to +3%). More than 120k staff had been trained on GenAI tools.
- **2024-10-30 (Q3 24):** The **FY24 target was cut again, to -2.0% to -2.4%**, and the margin range narrowed to 13.3–13.4%. This is the **first quantified GenAI disclosure: ~€600m of GenAI bookings in 9M24, about 3.5% of bookings**.
- **2025-02-18 (FY24):** Revenue was -2.0% cc; Q4 bookings were €6.8bn (book-to-bill 1.22), with "client decision cycles that remain long". **GenAI was close to 4% of FY bookings and ~5% in Q4**. FY25 guidance was cc -2% to +2%.
- **2025-04-29 (Q1 25):** Revenue was -0.4% cc. **Generative plus agentic AI was more than 6% of Q1 bookings**.
- **2025-07-07:** Capgemini **agreed to acquire WNS for $3.3bn in cash**. The deal was framed as building "Agentic AI-powered Intelligent Operations" (Digital BPS). WNS shareholders approved it on 2025-09-01, regulators on 2025-09-17, and it closed on 2025-10-17. Cloud4C, a hybrid-cloud business, was announced on 2025-08-26 and closed on 2025-11-03.
- **2025-07-30 (H1 25):** Q2 revenue was +0.7% cc. GenAI and agentic AI were **more than 7% of Q2 bookings**. FY25 guidance became -1% to +1%, and a €2bn share buyback was announced.
- **2025-10-28 (Q3 25):** Revenue was +2.9% cc, with North America accelerating. There were "robust deal wins in generative and agentic AI", and **GenAI/agentic AI was more than 8% of client bookings** (per the presentation). FY25 guidance was raised to +2.0% to +2.5% (about 2 points from M&A).
- **2026-02-13 (FY25):** Q4 revenue was +10.6% cc, including WNS and Cloud4C. FY bookings were €24.4bn (+3.9% cc, book-to-bill 1.08). **GenAI was more than 8% of FY bookings and more than 10% of Q4**. FY26 guidance was cc +6.5% to +8.5% (about 4.5–5 points inorganic), with a 13.6–13.8% margin.
- **2026-04-30 (Q1 26):** Revenue was +11.0% cc and bookings +6.2% cc. **GenAI plus agentic AI was more than 11% of Q1 bookings**, and clients were "gradually moving towards more agentic AI projects".
- **2026-05-27 (CMD, London):** The CMD was titled "poised to capture the full value of the Agentic AI revolution". The 2028 ambition is a 2025–28 cc revenue CAGR of +5.5% to +7.5% (about 2 points M&A). It also targets a new all-in headline margin up 130–150bp to 12.1–12.3%, and cumulative organic FCF above €6bn over 2026–28.
- **2026-07-30 (H1 26):** Revenue was +11.3% cc (Q2 +11.6%) and Q2 bookings €6.5bn (+9.2%, book-to-bill 1.07). FY26 cc growth was raised to about +8.5% to +9.0%. Agentic AI is "at the center of virtually every client conversation". No GenAI bookings share was disclosed this time. Q3 26 revenue is scheduled for 2026-10-30 at 07:00.

## Timing notes (events CSV)

- **2019-07 onward:** Times come from the publication timestamps on investors.capgemini.com, given in Paris local time. These match the WordPress `article:published_time`.
- **2016-10 to 2019-04:** Timing comes from the conference-call time printed in each release ("today at 8 a.m." means BMO; "today at 6/6.30 p.m." means AMC). The FY17 release (2018-02-15) had its call at 08:00, so it is BMO, even though the IR calendar shows 17:40. The H1 18 release (2018-07-26) had its call at 18:30, so it is AMC, even though the calendar shows 10:00. The IR calendar's "10:00 am" for pre-2019 events is a site default, and the 08:00 WordPress timestamps on pre-2019 posts are migration artefacts; neither is used.
- **2015-02 to 2016-07:** Timing comes from the earliest CercleFinance/BFM dispatch on tradingsat.com, which is an upper bound on release time. These were FY14 07:57, Q1 15 07:37, H1 15 08:12, FY15 08:01, Q1 16 07:52 and H1 16 08:11, so all are BMO; HH:MM is left blank.
- **Remaining UNKNOWN:** The 2015-10-29 Q3 release has no time evidence found. The CMDs on 2015-05-28, 2016-06-01, 2017-09-12 and 2018-10-30 have only same-day releases ("holding today"), so day 0 is the CMD date. The 2015 CMD release was filed with the AMF dated 2015-05-29.
- **2020-07-28 18:45:** "H1 2020 estimated results" is a preliminary release (prelim, AMC). The full H1 20 results followed on 2020-09-03.
- **Not in the events CSV:** Large M&A is outside the allowed event types. These are IGATE (2015-04-27, released with Q1 15), Altran (2019-06-24 18:34, AMC), WNS (2025-07-07 07:00, BMO) and the Capgemini Government Solutions sale (2026-09-12 16:00, intraday). The Atos-rumour denial is also excluded (2022-07-01 15:10).

## Sources and gaps

- **Sources:** investors.capgemini.com (calendar-past, event pages, financial-news, publication pages, PDFs at /en/file/<id>), and tradingsat.com dispatches for 2015–16 timing.
- **Documents:** Press releases from 2016Q3 to 2026H1, presentations from 2021Q3 to 2026H1, the 2021 and 2026 CMD decks, and the WNS deal releases. Capgemini publishes no call transcripts, only audio webcasts and CEO/CFO videos, so none are included.
- **KPI gaps:**
  - Q2 and Q4 revenue amounts are not stated in the H1/FY releases, so they are blank. Q2/Q4 rows carry only cc/organic growth, bookings and book-to-bill.
  - The H1 23 book-to-bill is not captured.
  - Bookings growth is not stated for FY24, Q4 24, Q4 25 and H1 26.
  - The Q2 26 bookings growth (+9.2%) has no stated basis.
  - Gross margin is not reported; operating margin is in `extra_json`.
  - 2026Q3 has not been published yet (scheduled 2026-10-30).
- **Harmonisation note:** The scheduled 2026-10-30 Q3 2026 revenue release (07:00, BMO) was dropped from CAP.PA_events.csv because it falls after the as-of date (2026-10-23). The blank 2026Q3 KPI row was also removed. CMD fiscal_period values were normalised to the event year.
